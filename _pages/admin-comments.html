Nothing a reader writes appears on the site until it is approved here. This replaced the Cusdis dashboard, which went away with the rest of Cusdis in September 2026. Comments now live in our own Netlify Blobs store and are read and changed through /.netlify/functions/comments. Gated behind the editor login — see _includes/admin-gate.html, which spells out what that does and doesn't do. Unlike /admin-stats/, the content of this page is NOT baked into the HTML: it is fetched afterwards from the function, which checks the Identity token for itself. So the page being served to anyone who knows the URL doesn't hand them the comments — they get an empty shell and a 401. That is the arrangement admin-gate.html describes as the real lock, and unapproved comments are the first thing on this site worth putting behind one. {%- endcomment -%} {% include admin-gate.html %}
