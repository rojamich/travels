{%- comment -%} ============================================================================= subscribe.html — the email sign-up form, on every page that has one ============================================================================= follow.it's embedded form. The reader types an address and subscribes without leaving the page; follow.it emails them when a post goes live, and the list is managed from the follow.it dashboard. Used by: index.html the homepage _pages/about.md the About page _layouts/post.html the foot of every post _layouts/trip.html the foot of every trip page The last two used to show a green button that opened follow.it's own page in a new tab. Same list at the other end, but it cost the reader two steps — click through, then type the address over there — and it took them off the post to do it. Here they type the address where they already are. WHY THE FORM HAS target="_blank" follow.it generates this form without one, and without one the browser navigates the whole page to follow.it's response. Which makes the form worse than the button it replaced: the button at least left the post open in the tab behind it. So the response opens in a new tab and the post she wrote stays exactly where the reader left it. That tab is not a throwaway, either — follow.it answers with "check your email to confirm", and a reader who never sees that sentence never finishes subscribing. It needs somewhere to be shown; it just does not need to be shown on top of the post. prompt optional line above the form. Posts and trips pass one, because "Enjoyed this?" only means anything directly after something she wrote. The homepage and About pass nothing and let follow.it's own heading do the asking. The styling lives in assets/css/main.scss under SUBSCRIBE BLOCK, not here. It is 3.4KB of follow.it's generated CSS, and inlining that into all ~300 post pages would be 3.4KB nobody's browser gets to cache. If follow.it ever hands you a new form, the markup below is the part to replace — check their CSS against what is in main.scss before pasting that too. ============================================================================= {%- endcomment -%}
