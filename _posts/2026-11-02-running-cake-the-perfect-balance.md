Our grocery shopping trip exposed us to a brand new oreo flavor: pancake! OH my goodness it was so good! I think I can still smell the maple syrup flavor even though those cookies are LONNNNNNG gone 😂🍪

Strolling around town, we noticed a pretty heavy-handed theme...

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1793663079/qxacup7wrb7fglmqsexq.jpg)

Apparently, Ushuaia was originally built as a penal colony! They established a high-security prison here in the late 19th century and it was thought of as "The Argentine Siberia" due to the impassable mountains and freezing seas! The prisoners here actually built the foundation of the city! In 1947 the prison was shut down due to human rights concerns.

We also notice these tickets on cars every day! We haven't seen any signs saying not to park here, but they're handing out tickets like crazy!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1793663083/frq1gsvya1uiraq34bqd.jpg "The ticket is for 300ARS which is less than .25¢ !")

Mike tried a new chicken sandwich for lunch and proclaimed loudly, "Oh no! It's not even chicken!"

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1793662913/hhhxoxi8hkjuo5qo6vnc.jpg "I wonder what gave it away?? He says it was still really good!")
