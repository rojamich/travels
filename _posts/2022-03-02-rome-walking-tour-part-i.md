The next day, all we had planned was a walking tour, provided by none other than Rick Steve lol. The first few sights were the Palazzo Farnese, three Baroque Fountains, and a recommendation for us to try Tartufo -an extremely rich chocolate gelato!

That chocolate was so rich and so dark it felt like I was punched in the tastebuds – in a fun way!

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676265/blogger-import/7eacb6f90e8879b7b2ca.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676276/blogger-import/b7e3aec29b9a06533a3b.jpg)

  


 We desperately needed water after that! We found out that the fountains around the city provided fresh, drinkable water from a two-thousand-year-old aqueduct! The water was so cold and refreshing. Here’s us excitedly trying it! We laughed saying it must be amusing for Romans to watch people excitedly try a water fountain. Imagine if someone took a selfie and admired the novelty of one of our water fountains lol.
