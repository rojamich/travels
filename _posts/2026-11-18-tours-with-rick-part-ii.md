![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795715842/jbl5avf19ivtodxo2aq8.jpg)

We headed out to the street where we learned that much of Madrid’s traffic is routed underground! Its SO nice how many streets here are preserved for pedestrians. Once again happy to not rent a car on these complicated roadways.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795715868/io9q9ttng7ohq0fswbld.jpg)

Behind the church sits the Royal Palace!

A Moorish castle was in this spot in the 800s, but in the medieval times, there was a Christian fortress! Before the final form you see now, it was a Renaissance palace. With 2800 rooms and sitting at 1.5 million square feet, its Europe’s largest palace! We had no idea the largest palace in Europe was in Spain 🤯
The view from the side, overlooking the city is endless! I was so happy to be reminded that it’s fall with crunchy leaves at our feet and a park filled with leaves changing color 😍

Following the major roadway here, we found a line wrapped around the royal palace and all the way down the road! If you are a resident, there is free entry to the palace from 4pm to 6pm. It was now 330pm, wow, they’ve been lined up awhile!

We were led to a peaceful plaza and despite walking through a busy city filled with millions of people, it was near dead-silent. We could hear only a violinist — that’s pretty impressive from a city the size of Chicago! And near a major landmark!

We were led back to our side of town, passing another neat roadway sign, a cute cookie shop, and more beautiful Christmas decorations!

On the walk, we learned that many international movies brought to Spain were edited for sexual references or liberal politics. Some of the movies today are still dubbed!

Rick led us to the San Gines church, one of the oldest in Madrid!

This street has a club on the left-hand side, a church on the right, and past the bookshop… down the alley is Chocolateria San Gines.

Here they sell churros and hot chocolate 24/7! They cater to A LOT of different clientel at different times of day. Party-goers at the wee hours, church goers in the morning and afternoon, and tourists at every hour in between

We saw they sold truffles and grabbed a few for Amazing Race night 🍫! We will catch up on episodes some day.. We missed a few on the cruise and have been so busy since, we haven’t gotten to watch!

Rick wrapped up our tour and we decided to try some gelato! You won’t believe what we found! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795716414/qnhxgxzmieutitmgbqj6.jpg)

As I stood waiting for our lunch, I spotted a gelato shop across the street. The name looked very familiar and to our great surprise, it was the same chain we walked 30 minutes to in Ushuaia! We didn’t even know it was a chain! They had Mike’s Dubai ice cream flavor but not mine. Thankfully, their peanut caramel gelato was really good too!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795716442/isx0cgcukrczuauvxnuf.jpg "Simon, the man working inside was even from Argentina!")

Later that evening, we headed out for coffee and to get some work done when we heard what sounded like a performance going on! We followed the sound and crowds and came upon this adorable little performance!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795716598/orwqffbpkufeu4xtugqa.jpg)

We got a lot of work done and headed off for dinner. I decided to try the Vietnamese place next door. I ordered and pronounced the dish correctly, which is tricky because it’s not how it looks spelling-wise and spoke some Vietnamese. The owner was visibly taken aback and pleased, asking why we speak Vietnamese!

We talked about the country and how she’s from Hanoi. Gosh I miss that country.  The food was really good and you can tell this because it ate it so quickly I didn’t get a photo. 

Mike tried a shop called Oink 🐷! And had a ham & cheese sandwich and said it was good. It being called oink reminded me of parks and recreation when the restaurants are called the lard bucket and the slop trough. 

With our window open, we were able to sleep a lot better this evening!
