This week has been pretty awesome! Aside from being sick the first few days, its been productive and we've had fun 😁

We have started a new Sunday tradition: Pastries & Pigskin where we eat pastries and watch football all day 😂🏈🥐

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789593110/wlpyeqip9tmk2okeaxu9.jpg)

We stopped by this bakery and decided to try a few Argentine pastries! 

* In the cup are Bizcochitos de Grasa which taste like lemon shortbread cookies.
* Medialunas de Manteca - golden croissants, coated in sugar
* Alfajor de Chocolate - tasted like a chocolate-dipped cake
* Factura con Crema Pastelera - caramelized pastry with custard on top
* Mini Alfajor - cookie with alfajor in the middle
* Medialuna de Grasa - another croissant but plain
* Vigilantes - puff pastry sticks with sugar sprinkled on them!

**My top three:**

1. Bizcochitos de Grasa
2. Vigilantes 
3. Medialunas de Manteca

**Mike's top three:**

1. Factura con Crema Pastelera
2. Medialunas de Manteca
3. Bizcochitos de Grasa

Can't wait to try more this weekend! Football was fun and we got an HDMI cord so we can have Redzone on the TV here 🥳

We found a new gym that's a 12 minute walk from us called Always Gym and its huge! Two stories high with free weights, machines, lots of cardio equipment, food and drink at the cafe inside, as well as workout classes. There are TONS of trainers walking around for assistance or to spot you. I was about to adjust a bench and it wasn't moving easily and a trainer was at my side before I even tried again! The downside is that it smells like coffee and waffles inside lol.

Speaking of, the streets here often smell like pastries and its hard not to have a pastry every time we walk outside lol. Our favorite pastry shop is literally on the corner next to our gym which feels unfair.
