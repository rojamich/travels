Its an early morning here in Madrid for the Rojas family as we make our way to the train station for the 620am train! It was about a 30 minute walk and we hustled over. We can’t tell… are the streets watered down at night for cleanliness or did it rain?? My money is on raining but Mike says cleaning. It’s both rained here off and on, and we’ve seen people hosing down the street outside their buisness. But the main road is so heavily “watered” that it’s filled with puddles!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795724001/wk3nqiylyswgy9kyc3qp.jpg)

The station was relatively empty at this hour and only one train was present and being boarded. We sat down stairs after security and waited… and waited… and started getting suspicious. Our train was in the station, no one was around us, and it was 10 minutes til departure. We got up to investigate and asked a security guard. He jogged to ask a colleague and spoke spanish, motioning to the far end of the hallway. We didn’t fully understand and walked that direction while searching. He shouted after us, “quince! Correo!” Meaning gate 15 and RUN! We hustled down the hallway (Mike with a boiling hot cup of coffee in one hand and a bag packed full of snacks in the other) and me with a backpack, towards the stairs. Because, of course it was stairs. We hit the second level and ran down the other side’s escalator to board the train. In our seats, the train started moving two minutes later. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795723998/ddrf5kjbueemw5ximmus.jpg)

We still aren’t totally sure why one train boarded at our seating level but ours boarded from the second floor…just to walk back downstairs! I worked on the blog and sorted photos from our trip to Antarctica! I have been very behind on the blogging thanks to all the seasickness and data limitations! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795724003/eb8uewd97x6b3luqgiqx.jpg)

It was a fun train ride! We loved it!
