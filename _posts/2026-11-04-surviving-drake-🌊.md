The first morning, Mike and I woke up feeling VERY seasick, even with our patches on 🤮! 

We made it through breakfast and began attending briefings — we learned about geology and birds found here in Antarctica! Between every class, we went back to bed. Horizontal was best for fighting the seasickness.

It’s very early in the season so we saw very few boats down here. Apparently, it gets very busy later in the season!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794069868/rodyqggvhdgtnrhpnl7z.jpg)

 I got sick the first day, but it was in our bathroom, thankfully. Many people didn’t make it that far, which is why the hallway railing holds many bags like this one.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794070366/afbxh2ohbmwx70mpijqd.jpg)
