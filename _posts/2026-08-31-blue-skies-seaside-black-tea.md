Looking out over the lower prayer room area, we learned about the history of this site:

* It was originally built as a Christian basilica between 532 and 537 AD ✝️
* The site was the worlds largest cathedral for over 1,000 years!
* Sultan Mehmed II converted the building into a mosque in 1453 🕌

We can clearly see this change later when looking at how the prayer niche is off-center to face Mecca!

The large black circles are called calligraphic medallions and are over 24 feet across 🤯 They have the names of Allah, Muhammad, and Muhammad's grandson's names. 

We learned this urn wasn't your typical urn. It actually used to hold water and sometimes sherbet on special occasions! Imagine not knowing if this is your ashes urn or sherbet urn –– what a fun way to keep people on their toes!
