The final day of the cruise arrived in a sudden flash! One evening we were barely climbing out of our beds to scrounge up dinner and the next morning we were preparing to disembark! There was no slow transition for us coming out of our trance-like states. We ate ravenously this morning, trying to regain what we'd lost and enjoyed a final meal with friends. Saying goodbyes and chatting about future plans, exchanging contact info, and talking about travel home. 

Breakfast was delicious and we headed for one final walk down the gangway. We were sad to say goodbye to our favorite crew and guides and gave Pippa a "Pippa Sandwich" where Mike and I hugged her — smushing her between us.

After grabbing our bags and designating our other two for storage, we walked back towards downtown Ushuaia. We quickly caught up with our adopted family and decided to spend the morning together! We all had time to kill and they showed us one of their favorite hobbies: geocaching! They patiently showed us the app and how to hunt down treasures all over the world! They have hundreds of accomplishments on there 🤯

We decided to track down one that was nearby and on the way to ice cream! They agreed to do our long walk over there and we were delighted to have a bit more time before saying goodbye. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795245068/ltuxpxgs4htpz0hyzofa.jpg "Elizabeth strikes gold! ")

We spread out, scouring the area for treasure when Elizabeth located the goodies! She displayed it all, cataloged it, and marked it as found in the app. What a fun side quest we got to be a part of 😃

Ice cream was well worth the walk, especially after not getting enough food the last few days!

We hugged and said our goodbyes 🥺 hoping we will meet again on another continent 🌏

The rest of the day was spent getting laundry done, watching football, and preparing for our flight the next day. We can't do many souvenirs but I found one small enough to pack!
