Up before any local food is open, we headed back to the “bagel” shop! Mike was blessed with endless patience with my need for protein, love for early mornings, and tireless want to return to the same places 😂

This time, I tried a jalapeno bagel and they did not skimp on the spiciness for this bagel, y’all. With our walking shoes laced up, we headed down to Gran Via — a massive thoroughfare in the city!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795721295/ag9kxarki7pkxsrxbpxp.jpg)

They had just about every major brand line you could think of here because it’s their version of 5th avenue! They even had a massive, multi-story bookstore here. I somehow resisted entering (I have one book I haven’t finished from the cruise because I was too sick) but the real reason is probably because I’m sick. If I ever skip a bookstore, that’s the likely cause. 

Mike created a budget app that helps us track every purchase to ensure we are below our targets 🤯 and we entered our purchases for the day while at a stoplight. We were well below budget for many of our trips thus far, wahoo! At the next block, I saw another advertisement for the Sagrada Familia Lego set… that cathedral isn’t even in this city! Wait.. should we do a spontaneous trip to Barcelona?? I have been DYING to see this cathedral, absolutely pining for it. It looks so GRAND in the photos and boom, it ended up on the bucket list. If you’re reading this post, stop and go look up a city view of Barcelona and how giant it is, in comparison to the rest of the city. 

We immediately went online to see how long the train takes…3.5 hours hmm… that means seven hours on the train in one day. Then onto the Sagrada Familia website, all sold out. Ok, I guess its not meant to be. We wouldn’t go all that way and all the time to miss out on Sagrada Familia. 

We continued our walk, seeing the Metropolis Building and the Bank of Spain which were grand and enormous! The dome one reminded us of the Gringotts bank in Harry Potter. Mike says it just needs a dragon on top 🐲

A long walk, a coffee, a colossal park with fall-colored leaves 🍂🍁… what else could I want??

We headed back to our place after a three-hour walk and were brought down this lane that is going to look insanely beautiful when they turn the lights on at night! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795723247/d2j7mhqap9wz16dgr1m8.jpg)

We were sparked with an idea…what if we tried a getyourguide tour or viator tour for tickets into Sagrada? They might have some left?! With incredible luck, one reasonably priced tour with good reviews had six tickets left… *Should we*??

Let’s double-check what time trains run, can we get back to Madrid in time to sleep, would we be late for our Sagrada entry if the train was delayed? Is it commonly delayed and by how much? How much is the train? All our research told us, take the first train, its commonly delayed but we’d likely be in before the tour, take the second-to-last train back, we’d still have enough time to rest before our train out of Madrid the next day. Plus, we didn’t have anything on the Madrid schedule for that day, it was a just-in-case day!

We snapped up those tour tickets so fast! Then booked the train in the quiet car both ways. HELL TO THE YES WE ARE GOING TO BARCELONA TO SEE SAGRADA FAMILIA ⛪💒 I could not be more ecstatic 🥳

For lunch, it was time to try a little of the local culture, and that meant heading back to the Ham Museum 🐷🐖🐽 We ordered their taster platter and decided to do a blind taste-test. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795720915/ovgyz2ovta9fqrvlnpmq.jpg)

We took turns covering our eyes, and the other person would select a ham for the other to try. The person would rate its taste and onto the next blind taste. I fed Mike first and he rated the hams in this order:

1. 75%
2. 50%
3. 25%
4. 100%

I rated them

1. 50% (actually really good, I don’t like ham but I’d eat this ham in a pinch).
2. 75%
3. 25%
4. 100%

The texture of the 100% is a no go. Cannot do it. So tough and lean. 100% ham means its made with purebred imberico pigs. Needless to say, we had A LOT of ham leftover after getting full on ham. We took the rest of it to go, for ham snack or sandwich later.

On the way back to our hotel, there was a protest going on in the Puerta del Sol, just like we’d learned about yesterday. This was an ongoing (44th day) public education strike, where teachers are demanding:

* Better funding
* Fewer students to teacher ratios
* And the resignation of the Education Minister

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795723245/cif48ktvfbxqayyccj8r.jpg)

Back at our hotel, we were able to do laundry for only 5euros, now THAT is a deal. 

Mike suggested ramen across the street for dinner.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795723370/o7yacddmanyaj4d1epxw.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795723389/mrwg2mbi37unpauidwk3.jpg)

Mine didn’t have broth and was really spicy with chicken, hard boiled egg, and chili sauce. Mike’s was also spicy with breaded chicken. Both were extremely good! We even got bibs to protect our clothing (turned out to be very necessary!)
