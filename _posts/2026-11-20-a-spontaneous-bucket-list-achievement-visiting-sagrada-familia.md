We scurried off to see the Sagrada Familia ahead of our tour. This is a bucket list item and deserves time to appreciate it in all its glory!

Stopped in our tracks, we took a moment to appreciate its beauty.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795806704/xwqtnamp1bnmn3flvg42.jpg)

Getting closer, the throngs of people grew thicker. This is what spontaneity, going on a weekend, and arriving post 9am gets you!

Still, worth every penny, every "disculpe" (excuse me), every walk sign, and every second it took to get here 😍

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807087%2Flicx7b80ryesv787bflx.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807087%2Fs5t7oxmrz5tznckqfcyt.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

The park surrounding this side of the basilica was filled with little market stalls. Some sold snacks, others sold Christmas knick knacks, and some sold straight souvenir items 🌲🎁🥞

Standing directly in front of Sagrada Familia, we felt so tiny! 

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807917%2Frduvnflajnve6wxfq1hr.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807916%2Fzauag70horgfdhsqxfrk.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Check out that level of detail in the four Evangelist Towers — those are the ones you see topped with orange, surrounding the one with a cross on top! Those are the details I really wanted to take time to take in. They're shaped like pine cones but I'm fascinated with them for some reason 🤔

We had to meet our guide at a shop nearby, so we headed there, able to take it all in along the way. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795808140/yijuoex3hunjb4sf77et.jpg)

Our group was about 15 people and filled with some interesting characters. Thankfully, our guide was very kind and passionate about the tour 📹

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795810164/ncf5e48mjdrxjz8sp4eb.jpg)

We started at the front of the church which has a large park in front of it and a huge line of people taking photos. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795812181/ekrxrvwntxmp610b7ze3.jpg)

There was time to snap a few shots before he got us into the Basilica 📷

Here is where he pointed out the representations in the architecture that Gaudi designed (yes, him again!)

* There are 12 Apostle bell towers surrounding the Basilica: One side's towers represent Nativity, one is for Passion, and one is for Glory. 
* The 4 Evangelist Towers we pointed out earlier represent Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
* The second-to-tallest tower is for the Virgin Mary and the tallest (172.5m aka 565.94 feet) is for Jesus Christ.

We were able to listen along on these headsets, which was great because it was quite loud with all the crowds!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795811302%2Fugtobmtah2teshkprugd.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795811302%2Fppli6ct1e9txhfrnklny.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

We learned that construction began on the church in 1882. Gaudi was not the original architect but took over because there was a bitter dispute over costs and materials with the first one. Adrian, our guide, shows us what the original creator envisioned with the Sagrada.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795812278/yefs9g3ymmg4bxiion5n.jpg "Lookin' real different these days 😂")

The second choice declined the job and recommended Gaudi! He only had five years of professional experience before taking over 🤯

This is the tallest structure in the city, intentionally. It also happens to be the tallest religious building in the world (as of February '26). It was supposed to be completed this year but is nowhere near ready. Here we can see the model, what is yet to be built is in tan. 

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795812363%2Fliwsalsyttum5puqjeke.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795812365%2Fyl4ci7800hfpcnq1tjtt.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795812366%2Fo2zuozmm8iabaxgoccos.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

I really hope we are alive to see it completed. Unless their goal is to become the longest, continuous, construction Basilica.

There was a Japanese chief sculptor working on the Sagrada named, Etsuro Sotoo and he intentionally added Japanese features in some of the statues to show that all races are universally accepted into heaven.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795812871/yvsz6stajaftb2qvdsy5.jpg)

These columns have ribbons spiraling around it with the names of the genealogy of Jesus.

Our tour guide joked that the mesh is to keep in the carved snakes from entering the church but it's really to protect them from tourists — pretty sad that that's necessary.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813148/byaywbz3r90q0yw3hipe.jpg)

The doors are designed with gorgeous ivy and filled with dozens of tiny creatures like ladybugs, bees, lizards, etc 🐞🐝🦎

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813236/qfhmybf4ocbis6a9d0nh.jpg)

As we walk through the entryway, we are dwarfed by the ginormous columns and can easily see Gaudi's intention of making the columns feel like trees in a forest.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813345/pmueqx6kpkxdv5wxuxpz.jpg)

See how the tops branch off like tree trunk branches?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813397/q193ow1fghmpqgfyr0xv.jpg)

See how the ceiling blossoms into flowers??

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813463/w7md2zlb8ajzs5fuapnt.jpg)

Something caught our eye as Adrian brought us further inside.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795813599/vnffryhsze5befeqokkh.jpg)

I was smitten and desperately excited to see the exterior of Sagrada but I had NO idea of the interior's beauty! ESPECIALLY how the sun filters in through them 😍🌈

We stood here, taking in it's beauty as Adrian explained that the rainbow-nature of the different stained glass symbolizes the cycle of the day and the life of Jesus.

Gaudi really thought of everything when he designed the columns to be perfect for leaning against to rest during worship.

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795813906%2Ffpfdmfnyxyfs4cdpch2w.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22Not%20a%20joke%2C%20that's%20really%20the%20intention%20behind%20the%20design.%22%7D %}

Gaudi died in June of 1926 — yep, that's 100 years ago this year! He was struck by a tram and received delay of his care in the hospital because his clothes made him appear homeless. Taxis refused to take him to the hospital but a police officer finally took him to a charity hospital where he died two days later. 

To celebrate the anniversary of his death, they had a Solemn Mass inside the Basilica where they also blessed the newly completed Tower of Jesus. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795814255/oxrverpyxhtvfh9q1xj6.jpg)

The stone wall you see before you is actually a set of doors! It says Our father in many languages and has the Lord's Prayer in Catalan. This door is solely used for Popes entering the church. Its currently closed off to tourists but, when completed, this will serve as the main public entrance!

Looking back towards the altar, you can take in how grand this building really is!
