We scurried off to see the Sagrada Familia ahead of our tour. This is a bucket list item and deserves time to appreciate it in all its glory!

Stopped in our tracks, we took a moment to appreciate its beauty.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795806704/xwqtnamp1bnmn3flvg42.jpg)

Getting closer, the throngs of people grew thicker. This is what spontaneity, going on a weekend, and arriving post 9am gets you!

Still, worth every penny, every "disculpe" (excuse me), every walk sign, and every second it took to get here 😍

The park surrounding this side of the basilica was filled with little market stalls. Some sold snacks, others sold Christmas knick knacks, and some sold straight souvenir items 🌲🎁🥞

Standing directly in front of Sagrada Familia, we felt so tiny! 

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807917%2Frduvnflajnve6wxfq1hr.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795807916%2Fzauag70horgfdhsqxfrk.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Check out that level of detail in the four Evangelist Towers — those are the ones you see topped with orange, surrounding the one with a cross on top! Those are the details I really wanted to take time to take in. They're shaped like pine cones but I'm fascinated with them for some reason 🤔

We had to meet our guide at a shop nearby, so we headed there, able to take it all in on the way.
