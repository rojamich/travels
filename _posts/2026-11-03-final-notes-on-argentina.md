We are so grateful to re-experience South America with this trip to Argentina! BA was a beautiful city filled with greenery and lots of parks! I loved that I had many running routes to choose from and there were pet dogs *everywhere*!

I was unsure of how it would stack up to other countries we have visited and two months was a long time to commit to a country we've never been to! Thankfully, it turned out great 👍🏻

Everyone was so kind and patient over our attempts at Spanish! Very few people we interacted with were fluent in English and we enjoyed practicing with others. 

The food here was ... fine. We aren't huge steak people, but the empanadas were good! The pastries and sweets more than made up for it 🥮🍪🥧🍦and I will really miss those churros! The tap water here is clean and tastes good!

Mike and I used the eSIM app: 4S esim here, which worked well everywhere we went! Wifi was intermittently available in restaurants/cafes but you usually had to ask for a password. 

We mostly walked here and occasionally used uber, but also got to utilize their buses 🚌! They were really cheap and easy which was great for our long transports across the city. We even used a ferry to get over to Uruguay for the day 🚢

If we returned, we would love to see more of the country. This is a massive country and we only had the chance to see two cities. More of Patagonia would be great too! Unfortunately, we couldn't see Iguazu falls on this trip because El Nino made it unviewable — the water levels were too high and the walkways were dismantled. If we go to Brazil before returning to Argentina, we would love to see it from both the Brazilian side and the Argentine side!

**Days visited**: September 9th, 2026 - November 3rd, 2026

**Daily weather**: Low 50°Fs- low 60s (10°C to 16°C) at night, 50s -70s (16–21°C) during the day in BA and rained a lot! In Ushuaia, Weather in late October–early November was  30s°F (-1 to 4°C) in the evening and 40s°F (4–9°C) during the day, and it was rainy intermittently.
