I woke up at 4am feeling great! (doesn’t get dark and portholes open). After no exercise the last two days, I was antsy. I headed to the top deck and admired the beauty! Then I found a spot that’s only mildly icy and danced. It began to softly snow <3

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794151022/ooalwxjpcetcwgoigbsc.jpg)

I went back out with my camera and got to see many birds (including penguins swimming!)

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1794150929%2Fjapo6bzb0sun2tdrxoxf.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2221%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1794151968%2Fv6utp8evcaae3u4br7bw.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Thankfully, we felt only mildly sick because we were signed up for the very first activity! Kayaking in Cierva Cove!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794151081/u0woknard8cihslzd1zo.jpg)

After a quick breakfast, we headed to the meeting spot to grab gear. Wow! What a view!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2273%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1794151038%2Fklmtlp0gv8lbinhu8gkv.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22If%20you%E2%80%99re%20wondering%2C%20is%20that%20a%20helipad%3F%20Yes%2C%20it%20is!%20Woah!%22%7D %}

We were given boots (similar to what we wear for scuba), a thick wetsuit, and matching jackets.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794151041/uttcflnbi3egilj7kwe2.jpg "One of my favorite colors, how cute is this??")

We were able to kayak with Elizabeth today and were excited to share this adventure together!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794151420/sem3m5go8qklipglgajf.jpg)

We climbed down the gangway steps to hop in the zodiac. It had several double kayaks trailing behind it.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794151692/niokk2ze4sexhta0txp1.jpg)

We sped out towards the glaciers and were soon surrounded by icy water that had icebergs floating in it!

{% youtube_block %7B%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fyoutube.com%2Fshorts%2FQtEIukEEwKk%22%7D %}

This is a video a girl in our group took of a clear iceberg floating in the water 🧊

The guides helped us to carefully slide into our kayaks to avoid premature polar plunges 😂


{% youtube_block %7B%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2FqkSsAwqw58g%22%7D %}

And we were off, following our guide, Alexis. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794152390/qllm7vmhr6bpliwuj2rt.jpg)

It was unbelievable experience to slide through ice to move the kayaks. Our paddles slipping into the water and maneuvering around blocks of ice. The sound was exquisite. Hearing nothing else but our kayaks moving through the freezing landscape was so peaceful. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794152857/hf06lhfi0ruhjkpsy9of.jpg)

We got to see many different icebergs with varying hues of blue! The more compressed, the deeper blue they look!


![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794154324/sdxugoi2strgqrjraseq.jpg)

We were visited by penguins, majestically swimming through the ice!

{% youtube_block %7B%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2F5SEMjxpB398%22%7D %}

And we even saw a Weddell seal hopping back into the frigid water!


{% youtube_block %7B%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2F4MYGURpUhBo%22%7D %}

What an amazing start to this expedition! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794155156/kjvgxxz38age0dz0vr2v.jpg)
