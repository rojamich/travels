Costa Rica was on my MUST visit list and with my birthday coming up, we decided it would be a great time to see central America for a first trip there 😍 I'd always wanted to have a birthday party that was beach-related but with a birthday in February, that wasn't really practical...

We drove up to Portland the night before for an early flight to LA the next morning!

There was a ramen spot right next to Powell's bookstore and we soaked up both 🍜📖

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790098472/moi3iwfnsvrwkurlxi7a.jpg "It was... fine.")

But Powell's bookstore never disappoints 😍 walking up and down each aisle in the mystery/thriller and fiction sections is something I still daydream about 💭

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790098446/n7nj6cmighb7soel5mxg.jpg "We got to watch the sunrise on our way to LA :)")

It was a surprisingly easy set of flights, PDX to LAX, LAX to Guanacaste Airport (Liberia - LIR) with a total of about six hours flying! That's as long as it takes to get across the entire US and I can see a new country doing it? Obviously, sign us up! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790709789/b0p6hnmxivs4kbcywpwy.jpg "And what a treat to see the sunset heading to Costa Rica too!")

We got in some good reading time and I was trying out a classic, recommended by my dad: [Great Expectations](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/2623.Great_Expectations) by Charles Dickens. Interesting story!

It was a two-hour drive into Tamarindo from there — google maps will lie and tell you a 1.5 hour drive but the roads aren't exactly easy here (one of the reasons we didn't get a rental car!) But the transfer service was excellent and comfortable with water provided, yay!

We arrived to Tamarindo to warm weather at 9pm! This time of year its typically around 31-32°C (88-90°F) and nighttime lows dipping to about  23°C to 24°C (73°F to 76°F).
