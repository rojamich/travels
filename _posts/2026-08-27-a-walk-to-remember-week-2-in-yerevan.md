The first is a Khorovat (Armenian BBQ)- lule kebab which is ground lamb, and the second is called sarma which is grape leaves wrapped around rice, fresh herbs, spices, and ground meat.

Here is our ranking for all the Armenian food we tried:

1. Mante - meat dumpling soup 🍲
2. Khorovat - ground lamb kebab 🍢
3. Muhammara - dip made of walnuts, red bell peppers, pomegranate molasses, and breadcrumbs 🥜
4. Lavash - flatbread, good but not as good as naan 🫓. 
5. Mutabel - tastes like garlic hummus🧄! 

   112. Sarma - grape leaves wrapped around rice, fresh herbs, spices, and ground meat🍇
