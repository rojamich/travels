We ordered a blueberry and a classic cinnamon roll, they were SO soft!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789920829/eew6dbne3rlvcvddasqj.jpg)

My mouth is watering just thinking about these! I love blueberry things. Oddly, I don't love eating straight-up blueberries.

We headed to a local coffee shop but it wasn't very good.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789920776/b6wk8nohtlspgylphjjk.jpg "Cute sign though!")

We fell back on our ol' reliable coffee drive thru by the house called Backwoods coffee! They're great at coffee and super sweet :) Highly recommend if you're ever in the neighborhood!

The Airbnb host had cut firewood for the stay but we were already running low because the biggest appeal of this spot was the fireplace! So, we learned how to be lumberjacks 🪵🪓

We enjoyed the day, playing games and reading books. I also finished up all my grading for term! I was reading [The Institute](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/43798285-the-institute) by Stephen King which was SO good!! I love that book. Definitely going on the tattoo. 

That night I found a line dancing spot in town called the Spur and it was having R&B night — so all the line dance songs were R&B or Rap themed, I was super excited because I love country but mixing it up is awesome. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789922922/nl2me78cjwhz4ibenrnr.jpg)

I learned a handful of songs and Mike supported me 😍🥰

Being out on the town helped us to stay up til it was dark enough to see the Northern Lights!

We drove out of town to see better and it was REALLY cold out there ❄️🥶

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789923193/en7c97pddcyqevsdka12.jpg)

They were really hard to capture between camera settings, freezing fingers, and other cars everywhere!
