Another fun week in Tbilisi as we have been exploring and simultaneously productive! Jenna finished all her fall prep for classes next term (what a relief!) Mike scheduled out all our day trips and the upcoming trip to Stepantsminda - the mountains of Georgia! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1785261897/20260725_143625_mqtofh.jpg)

We had the absolute best cantaloupe of our lives - so juicy and tender that my mouth drools thinking about it!

The power has gone out and the gas a couple of times but thankfully they were brief! The police are actually [investigating ](https://oc-media.org/georgia-investigating-nationwide-blackouts-as-acts-of-sabotage/)the power outages!

Did you know that some areas have signs to let drivers know that this is a high traffic area for the visually impaired!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1785263709/20260725_125340_aqr2ss.jpg)

I had my first dentist appointment overseas - tried to get it done in Corvallis before we left but they cancelled last minute dang it!

Churchkhela is their "Georgian Snickers" which is misleading because it is nothing like Snickers. That's not a bad thing because I don't like snickers either lol. Each flavor is a different juice (red wine juice, pomegranate, grape fruit, etc) poured thick over nuts! It was odd but not bad for me. I would eat it again if I had to since it's super rude to reject food here. I had a blast buying it! We have shops about 1 minute from our place and one is a produce shop. I asked him, what are the flavors and he RACED over to the different options and explained each of them. One, he couldn't remember the English word for it so he ran (literally ran) across the store to his magnet collection and pointed at the pomegranate 🤣😍 That might be the most fun I've ever had grocery shopping.

Our Airbnb host dropped off new linens for the week - we heard knocking at the scheduled time (down to the minute!) and opened the door to a truly jovial man (I need you to read this in the voice of Gru from Despicable Me.) who shouted:

> HELLO!!! MY NAME IS GARYYYYY!
>
> I DO NOT SPEAK ENGLISH!!

We have been saying this to each other for days. Needless to say, I'm getting caught up on the minion movies (did you know there are 5 of them now??) Each is as good as the first and I'm loving it. Why are those little yellow guys so amusing to me?? Mike also convinced me to watch the Planet of the Apes movie and it surprisingly was not bad. 

There has been lots of time for working out and reading: I've finished [Timeline by Michael Crichton](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7669.Timeline) and [Nora Goes Off Script by Annabel Monaghan!](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/58988426-nora-goes-off-script)

There is also endless Khinkali and several naps have taken place. What a week!
