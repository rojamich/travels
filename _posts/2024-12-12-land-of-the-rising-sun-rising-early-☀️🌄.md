As soon as we entered the Tokyo airport, it was an entirely different world!

We strolled through the crowds and found the one spot we have heard so many great things about here ... 7/11! That's right, the convenience store! It is nothing like US 7/11 with fresh foods, healthy meals on the go, and unbelievable snacks. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787501994/blogger-import/b1b7b3de52d477683cc2.jpg)

I went with something safe for my first try and found a cross between french fries/crackers/potato chips: the flavor was sour cream and pepper!

We got our Suica cards (for the metro) and hopped aboard to head to the heart of Tokyo and our hotel: Shibuya station!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787497201/blogger-import/b32ed2483c7386ce3e55.jpg)

Here we are completely overwhelmed with happiness to be back on train travel and finally in Japan! Train travel is undeniably my favorite form of travel. I would give up driving forever if I could train travel everywhere. 

The cool thing here is they love their vending machines - they are everywhere and not just drinks! Here is an ice cream one and we've seen ones with meat in them!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787506658/blogger-import/0e31c3cdf23598399c5e.jpg)

We navigated the metro system well thanks to Mike's research and passion for learning languages! Immediately we were greeted in Shibuya with so many signs vying for our attention and the smell of incredible food at every street corner. 

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1787496389%2Fblogger-import%2Fb06b6d7be8dadb44a798.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

There is an elevated walkway above part of the city which helps mitigate the street traffic vs pedestrian traffic!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787506012/blogger-import/3ca497149e9c41961185.jpg)

Our hotel was only about 10 minutes away and we were surprised by a beautiful sunset and view :)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787511444/blogger-import/992f881b9fe13a41f616.jpg)

After freshening up, we headed out in search of food to prevent my hanger and were quickly swallowed by the crowds of this bustling city!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1787496323%2Fblogger-import%2F89aaa450dd391eb5463c.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

We found the best ramen we have ever eaten in our lives and wished they had bottomless bowls to keep refilling.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787496487/blogger-import/0f15a47350b097677745.jpg)

We flanered about the city to admire their Christmas lights and take in the views. Oh, Christmas music was on every flight, throughout the airports, and in stores/restaurants.

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1787492931%2Fblogger-import%2Ff7ead3949685c215415c.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

We admire the love of rules here, much like Germany. There is an order to things and people generally stay within the cultural norms (minus some tourists).

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787498025/blogger-import/c4782c6091849425a69d.jpg)

Stay to the left on escalators unless you're walking up on the right! Cars drive on the left here and you generally walk on the left side.

Finally, we were dog-tired and ready for sleep. We slipped into our hotel-provided, Japanese-style jammies and passed out at 8pm!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787511846/blogger-import/5780ce74bf9d631bd511.jpg)
