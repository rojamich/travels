The last day in Stepantsminda sped by in a blur! I danced and grabbed breakfast at our favorite cafe while reading [Golden Son](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25372230-golden-son) (the second book in the Red Rising series). The series has been so good so far! I sat outside, eating in the lovely sunshine (its much cooler up here!) when I felt something touch the back of my leg, DANG BUGS! I went to brush it away but nothing was there? Then something touched my leg so I looked down again to the CUTEST SIGHT!!

Naturally, I stayed there petting him long after my food was gone 🥰

The evening after our hike, Mike tried the pork kebabs and I got dessert! It was a glorious brownie 🤤 Mike says his meat was very good!

This morning, we woke to a power outage! Thankfully, the windows are large enough to fill the room with sunshine so we could pack! Then, we discovered, one light was still working. That light turned off right as we finished packing, whew!

After breakfast, our driver Aleks picked us up for the transfer back to Tbilisi! He was excited and so sweet! We passed the first hour chatting about Georgia and Armenia (he's Armenian and we are going there tonight!). Before we knew it, we were at our first stop with a view ⛰️
