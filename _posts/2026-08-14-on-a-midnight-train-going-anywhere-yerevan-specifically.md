Our time waiting at Starbucks was up and we both had taken turns enjoying a delicious sushi dinner - here's Mike's!

Naturally, it was pouring rain with lightning and thunder! We caught a bolt to the train station and immediately were able to board! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786884527/20260814_214540_olpf90.jpg "Run! It's pouring!")

We boarded on the #11 car and walked through every train car to get to #2 - we booked a 1st class sleeper car so we didn't have to share a room 🛏️🚂

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786884586/20260814_215143_bn3dps.jpg)

The seats fold up to make bunks!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2249%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786884737%2F20260814_220028_qaxxpv.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786884754%2F20260814_223111_st37db.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Cozy and unpacked, we showed our tickets to the attendant who then brought us sheets/pillow covers.

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2283%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786885139%2F20260814_224327_pampnq.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786885141%2F20260814_224314_tuitp3.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

I immediately passed out, trying to get as much shut eye as possible before the border stops!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786885266/20260815_015014_xgfktb.jpg)

The first happened just before midnight. We all off-loaded the train and lined up for passport control. I was very smiley which made the border officer laugh and he had to ask me to stop smiling for the photo lol! He said, "oh, second time to Georgia!" and seemed to approve. Just wait til next year when we come back for a third trip! we got our exit stamps and re-boarded.

The second border stop happened around 130am and Armenian border patrol boarded the train. They seemed disinterested in American tourists but happy with our "barev dzez" which is a polite hello 😊

We fell right to sleep after that and slept until 8 when the attendant came to wake us (minus a brief wake up to see the sun rising!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786887307/20260815_063529_uiffvm.jpg)

The train station was surprisingly calm and we weren't bombarded with aggressive taxi drivers, wow!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2261%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786887350%2F20260815_090049_pkkpzm.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786887350%2F20260815_090056_dy0v7g.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

There were so many GG options (that's their Uber) for different types of rides and shipping 😱

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786948612/Screenshot_20260815_194411_gg_ymzqm2.jpg)

Mike found us a coffee shop near our airbnb to enjoy until check-in which was great because I was hungryyyyyyy!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2283%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786887429%2F20260815_094649_slj2ck.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2248%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1786887425%2F20260815_093621_tddabp.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

The food and coffee were delicious! They even made my iced caramel latte the way I like it! I hadn't had one for awhile so I felt like I was on speed 😂

Thankfully, our Airbnb was ready early so we packed up and left the coffee shop as a bro financial - MLM scam got underway 😱

The walk was pretty!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786948214/20260815_103954_aebbyl.jpg)

Of course, because the host was waiting for us to arrive, the Airbnb was difficult to find and it felt like the universe was mocking us.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786948479/20260815_105311_tyc2wf.jpg "This building was next door.")

Our host met us downstairs, thankfully and led us inside! It's an awesome place, super comfortable and really big compared to what we are used to! This is our view from the balcony!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786948560/20260815_113131_meipov.jpg)

We immediately noticed the AC remote wasn't working and messaged the host who got us fixed up right away. Thank goodness because I would melt and die without it!

Lunch was a 20 minute walk so we decided to head out and see some of the city and eat good food.

The bill came in this cute little jewelry box 😋

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786949174/20260815_154215_yeol3y.jpg)

Later that night, we decided to give a Vietnamese restaurant a shot - they did NOT disappoint! We were the only white people in the restaurant and got to practice our Vietnamese which was praised by the staff and we were so excited to be back in our favorite element! There were big groups of people there enjoying the food and giving us curious glances.

I got a cà phê muối (salted iced coffee) and cơm ba chỉ rang (pork belly and rice), Mike got a cà phê sữa đá (iced coffee) and Phở Bò (beef pho)!

Heading out, we saw this cool church all lit up and decided we had enough energy from the coffee to go grocery shopping. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1786952488/20260815_213625_gzblll.jpg)

There are much fewer signs at the grocery store in English too which made it challenging and I feel bad for all immigrants and travelers who don't speak English. We are fortunate that many countries use this on signs, instructions and everyday life. 

We were concerned to see they didn't have ground beef! I asked the gentleman using google translate app and he pointed to a steak. We said hmmm.. Then we realized he had a beef grinder right behind him!
