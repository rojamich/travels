Georgia has held such a special place in my heart for many years now and I am ever-so-grateful that I finally got to share this awesome country with Michael! We climbed to the top of the mountain that the Gergeti Trinity Church sits upon 🏰, gazed at the Friendship Monument, crossed the Bridge of Peace🌉, and hiked to Mother Georgia. All things I fantasized of showing him one day 😍

He loved khinkali 🥟 as much as I hoped he would and, the stinker he is, pushed me to try more and more new things! We dunked bread into the cheesy adjarian khachapuri 🍳, boiled in a sulphur bath 🛁, gave fake names at Starbucks ☕(what a weird bucket list item), and tried new recipes I would've previously balked at 🫘!

There were many adjustments to be made here to get into our new lifestyle and we met them head on 💪🏻Gym was outdoors, grocery shopping twice a week with a small fridge, walking/using public transportation regularly, and learning new cultural norms. 

Mike and I used the eSIM app here, which worked well! Wifi was available in some cafes and restaurants.

We utilized metros, buses, lots of walking, and bolt! 

Number of miles walked in:

Tbilisi > 147.22 miles

Stepantsminda > 21.4 miles

Total Miles Walked in 32 days is 168.62 miles!

That’s like walking from Atlanta, GA to Augusta, GA!

OR

Portland, OR to Eugene, OR!

OR 

San Diego, CA to Palm Springs, CA!

Almost *exactly* the same as Norway!!
