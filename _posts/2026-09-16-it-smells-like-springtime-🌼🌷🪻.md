I'm not sure how else to explain it but it smells like springtime here! I'm immediately joyful over the weather, friendly people, and feeling of being here.

We arrived outside our apartment building which our driver even pointed to (ensuring we didn't get lost, ow sweet!) and our host (who speaks Spanish only) came outside to greet us warmly 🤗. We are on the 6th floor so it's time to see what the elevators are all about here...

There are two doors to close and open before and after operating the elevator. It felt so old-fashioned and neat! There are three banks of elevators here. One will very suddenly jolt upwards and downwards and took some getting used to lol. Probably isn't helping things that Mike was recently stuck in an elevator!

I promised to tell you the differences in the apartment! One is that instead of H & C on the sink handles its "C & F" for caliente and frio! It has amazing blackout shades that you hand crank to close and it seriously blacks out the rooms. You must turn on the gas to start the stove and she emphasized that hot water is automatic – we hadn't realized that some were not automatic! There is also a separate toilet for a bidet. Aside from the natural light, the balcony is big and straight up awesome, we love it! There is also a good-sized bathtub that I've already go to soaking in like a bag of black beans 😂🫘

We got settled in and set out to explore the neighborhood! Wow, it's so colorful and filled with delicious treats!
