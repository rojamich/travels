It was time to enter the iconic Blue Mosque of Istanbul! Did you know that it was controversial for it's 6 Minarets?Apparently, it's abnormal to have more than 4! When it was constructed in the 1600s, this was seen a major act of pride and blasphemy 🫢

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788350331/rwyjspldipvvi9y9y0z4.jpg)

Shoulders, legs, and head covered? Check, check, and check! Shoes off? Oh yes👞👞👟👟. We were ready to enter this historic site 🕌
