That night, we were entertained by a different type of city walk offered by Rick Steve… The Redlight district! I was very surprised to see a whole store offering nothing but weird and different condoms! They were closed…so I guess no safe sex for anyone this evening lol. 

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785679368/blogger-import/0acae4ed4cdf85840019.jpg).jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785679377/blogger-import/c135ae8e97c8f9343a4e.jpg).jpg)

  


Next to a church, there was a statue dedicated to the unknown sex worker. Later in the evening there were streets filled with windows facing the road and women in skimpy clothing waiting for an offer from a john behind them.

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785679386/blogger-import/40cd82bb9623d3fb8877.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785679395/blogger-import/179e13ca135143a9f60b.jpg)

  


The last site on our tour was to walk by the Bulldog which is a popular weed café across town.

It was definitely a thought-provoking experience and we learned a lot about this culture. I thought it was interesting that crime went down after they legalized marijuana and prostitution.
