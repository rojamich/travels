Holy crap its so cold. No spoilers because you probably already knew it gets cold in Alaska in March! We ended up taking two days to get to Fairbanks, Alaska, by design. 

We stopped by Jenna's school to rent the snowshoes for about $14 instead of the $60 it would've been in Alaska 🤯

We also took time for our favorite sushi spot in Portland (shout out to Soku Sushi!) and grabbed sees candy for a snack to enjoy during our bunkering in a freezing cold state 🍫

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789767283/lnoomue2rsres1wuphfi.jpg)

Naturally, we stopped at Powell's bookstore because, who could resist the book shopping sweetness?? I've been resisting buying new books because we are moving in June so I go there and browse and add books to my TBR list. I always make sure to search Libby to see if they're on my library before buying too. The libraries I have on there are surprisngly well-stocked! I also use a website that alerts me if my TBR books that aren't in the library go on sale online for $1.99 or under!
