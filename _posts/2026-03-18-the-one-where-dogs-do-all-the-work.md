Today we were in for a bucket list item ✅!! I've always wanted to dogsled and now that we were finally in Alaska, it was time to!

Just seeing the signs leading up to the dogsledding kennels were exciting!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832005%2Fthuc6m5xtrjuqlbwca4r.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832002%2Fw0aea6ohj1pwsdxtfulh.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

We raced indoors to add even MORE layers to our already layered bodies 🥼🧥

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789832083/svdfax3ittzftk5gsurd.jpg "Gettin' a little toasty in here, y'all.")

Decked out, we were able to go meet their dogs! Some of these are even used in their Iditarod competition! Others are training to be. Some of them aren't here because they were currently competing in the [Iditarod](https://iditarod.com/)!

{% youtube_block %7B%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2F6-63sqjlyzY%22%7D %}

We were allowed to pet them and get to see their different personalities! And different they certainly all were 🤯

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832355%2Fuppznblbn03j9evxu2bc.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832344%2Fvuyzk4wcp6qznpx6mle8.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

The crew covered who we would be sledding with and even taught us how to harness them up 🪢

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832453%2Frnabvleoyhkmfjko4bga.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832450%2Fuslhy25hpy6t7hkagrw4.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2249%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832509%2Ftjtd1lcba42fwlnxegw3.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22I'm%20so%20happy%20I'm%20jumping%20for%20joy!%22%7D %}

The ride was so freaking cool. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789832456/z3euonbpfpq0x0lqvylf.jpg)

Our awesome instructor taught us every detail of turning, stopping, slowing, and how to avoid falling off (which is really common!)

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2249%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832578%2Ft6puxxqsxcvhvse4mrcv.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%22100%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832582%2Fc8m0xq6mzrvhcd11hobe.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%22100%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1789832593%2Fng4axb7uqri7iv5ydezo.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22Its%20so%20cold%20and%20I%20can't%20feel%20my%20fingers!%22%7D %}

It wasn't as fast moving as I thought it would be! We also didn't see a moose yet which was good under these conditions. They have a crew member riding their snow mobile ahead to make sure there aren't any on the trail (apparently there was on the morning before!)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789832700/x2mdjyfbbgyihxofri4f.jpg)

It was over entirely all too soon and we had to say our goodbyes to the babies 🐶😪

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1789832712/tirveccvropjz1gst9p9.jpg)

That night we played scattergories together and read books by the fire — it was peaceful and I'm so happy!
