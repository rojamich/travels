The sections highlighted many beautiful areas of the island that intrigued us and inspired us for future journeys. It blew me away. There was also a video game section where you could play Nintendo, race cars, and PlayStation. There was even a surfing area to test your skills (see the entirely blue photo with the surfboard on the ground and rails!)

Truthfully one of the best parts for me was this informative sign. 

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697228/blogger-import/476eacc119de2c504c30.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697232/blogger-import/c72449d3bf31ff70b9a5.jpg)

This information needs to be everywhere.

We cozied up in the lounge while eagerly awaiting our 2-hour flight from Taiwan to Japan. We enjoyed snacks, playing our NYT games (the app), reading, and catching up on the blog! We even tried Taiwanese Beef Noodle soup!

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697237/blogger-import/b8d40d14029c4c5a92ce.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697241/blogger-import/d7292ee8c4714a3f5713.jpg)

It was finally time to board and we headed downstairs to our gate. Moments later, about 100 middle school-aged children (not an exaggeration) came downstairs for what appeared to be a school field trip. These were some of the quietest kids we have ever encountered and, while the room was packed, we could still whisper-talk and hear each other!

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697246/blogger-import/bcf3b6fc9961aff8a5be.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785697249/blogger-import/45d5412616e8a9ef1c92.jpg)

We settled into our cozy chairs and resumed reading! It was a nice and easy flight and we were able to stay awake to get on the correct time zone and avoid jet lag.

We chose from two different menus this time, one Western option and one Japanese option. Mike was more adventurous on this flight and I played it safe. 

*Mike's Menu Options:*

Cashew arrowroot tofu with karasumi and wasabi.

Spinach, crown daisy, pickled chrysanthemum flower, candied chestnut, steamed carrots, deep-fried shrimp ball, grilled taro with cod roe, stewed autumn mushrooms, grilled chicken with cheese.

Marinated fried autumn veggies, grilled salmon and Chinese yellow chive, wagyu beef, mushroom rice, and mushrooms with miso soup.

*Jenna's Menu Options:*

Scallops and cod roe with pickled tomatoes, cheeseballs with fig filling, and seasonal salad.

Pan-fried rosemary chicken thigh with paprika cheese sauce, mushroom penne, zucchini, and cherry tomatoes. 

Jasmine tea orange bread and soy sauce bread. 

I rotated between the salad, penne, and bread to keep eating. But Mike thought it was good!

Mine was Chestnut Montblanc with blueberry jam and fresh fruits (the best cantaloupe I've ever eaten! The Montblanc was AMAZING.

One more book read completely and I'm quickly burning through my well-stocked Kindle supply! We touched down at Narita Airport with surroundings similar to the U.S.
