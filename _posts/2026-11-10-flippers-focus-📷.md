I woke up feeling very short of breath this morning and my sore throat had gotten worse. Do you think it had anything to do with jumping in freezing waters yesterday? Probably not. 

We were landing on Danco Island today, where we had tried to land earlier in our adventure but there was too much ice to access it! It was a gorgeous day and we were grateful for the second chance. Pippa seemed really excited about this spot too.

We lined up for the zodiacs and were surprised by another whale sighting! It was too far to photograph so we sat back and enjoyed watching it shoot water into the sky 🐳

There were tons of gentoo penguins on the island and we think they are so darn cute!

I didn't have my camera (naturally) but Pippa and I were able to sit there and enjoy the gorgeous gift. She said it was a baby!

By some miracle! Mike saw it from above!!
