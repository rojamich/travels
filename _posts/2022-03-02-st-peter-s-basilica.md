We had to walk out and around to get to Saint Peter’s Basilica where a long line snaked out front. There was a dress code of no shorts or exposed shoulders to enter. The exterior was massive and surrounded by the architect’s top 140 favorite saints. Until this trip, I did not know there are literally thousands of saints.

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676504/blogger-import/b7b628fab30070be42d1.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676522/blogger-import/9f757713772feeb31b9a.jpg)[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676526/blogger-import/2f94b9815b4d48011106.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785676535/blogger-import/d024deadafd4c39af189.jpg)

  


When we got inside, we were impressed! The ground had markings of other basilicas around the world and where they would fit _inside_ the Saint Peters! There was also the remains of a popular, previous Pope with a wax covering over his face. Mike was very impressed by how well he was preserved.
