From Lake Sevan it was an easy drive through the mountains to Dilijan and our hobbit hole stay for the evening! The forest was so dense it felt like something we would see in Africa with each tree awning covering it's neighbor! The switchbacks were crazy winding back and forth to come into the town and there was hot corn at *each. and. every. turn.*

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787824722/20260825_150625_ot2zwl.jpg "That's a lot of corn!")

We followed the turn of the main road and were thrilled to see this beautiful parking lot! *Have I ever said those words before??*
