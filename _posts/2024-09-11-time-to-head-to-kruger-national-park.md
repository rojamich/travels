After another delicious breakfast at "Nice" we packed up and headed to the airport for our quick 2 hour flight from Cape Town to Kruger National Park! Check out how cute and tiny this airport is!

We flew from the small blue/green circle at the bottom left point of Africa to the upper right-hand corner of South Africa with the darker blue spot (current location). 

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785692687/blogger-import/4defeac532f0509fca85.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785692692/blogger-import/f25aca532e673bc074f5.jpg)[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785692707/blogger-import/1ce38b4f215241c0a10b.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785692715/blogger-import/c7d29b9a46891ee9e2ca.jpg)

We had arranged for a transfer from the airport which took us to our hotel for the evening. On the way we saw this beautiful purple and multiple Impala!  

During the drive, I could see the GPS and at one point the road stopped showing signs for our hotel. I also noticed the driver was not following the GPS and it was indicating a U-turn. *Enter internal nervousness*. After about 2 miles of that, he returned to normal signages and the gps gave him a new route. Panic avoided! Mike had just shown me the movie called Hostel, and if you've seen it, you'll know what I'm thinking!
