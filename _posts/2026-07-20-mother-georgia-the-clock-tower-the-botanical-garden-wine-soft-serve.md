I have been so excited to hike up to Mother Georgia with Mike as it was one of the few activities I got to do my first trip here. Sharing that with him is so rewarding!

Mother Georgia is also called "Kartlis Deda" and is a massive statue overlooking Tbilisi - she was erected for Tbilisi's 150th anniversary! She is holding a bowl of wine for friends but a sword for enemies ⚔️🍷

She greets us in Georgian, asks if we speak Russian, German, Italian, or French! Mike enjoyed speaking some German with her and I am blown away by her ability to speak so many languages! 

Leaving the church, we decide the best way to see Mother Georgia from our current position is to explore the Botanical Garden first!

It was $2.50 for us both to enter the lush and sprawling garden that seemed to go on forever.
