We woke up with an alarm this morning to catch the first ferry out of Buenos Aires and were treated to this glorious and colorful sunrise!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790461976/e0pnqr39hwzumdpwtmk7.jpg)

We caught an uber for a 30 minute ride to the ferry where we would walk through security, and line up for passport control for both countries on this side! That was pretty unusual for us, usually each country does their own exit and then entrance but we exited Argentina and entered Uruguay on the same side with two different lines. Very smooth with limited questions! We grabbed a ferry snack and headed to another line to board the ferry 🛥️

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790461980/l8ydidixohtfpsg33uzz.jpg)

The ferry was super clean, had lots of seating, and even food and drink onboard! We grabbed a seat and set to reading Jurassic Park. I napped on and off too 🥱

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790461983/yr1v13o7qtf7kkk2ypbu.jpg)

It was a really fast ferry ride (one hour and 15 min) and BOOM, welcome to Uruguay! Our first stop was only a two minute walk away...

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790461987/mlqo5td0axerwltqotyx.jpg)

We walked past the Ministry of Public Health in Colonia del Sacramento, Uruguay and noticed they have a pride flag up! It seems like many countries we have visited so far are pro LGBTQ+ 🏳️‍🌈

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790462096/xhycweisaqq9ea0pqbzy.jpg "pride flags")

We loved the super historic feel of this town and we learned that it was founded in 1680 by Portuguese soldiers. I was stoked to walk across this moat/draw bridge because it was so beautiful and made me feel like I was transported into medievil times!

We walked along the lit, historic streets one last time and were grateful we decided to take the last ferry out of Uruguay. We never felt like it was too much time here and we really soaked up every minute!
