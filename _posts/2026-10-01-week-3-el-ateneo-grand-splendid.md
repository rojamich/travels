We have been really enjoying all the cold weather here — today was a high of 15c (59F) and the next week has a high of 21c (70f)! Yes, please and thank you. After a very toasty summer, we are happy to be back where we turn on the heater sometimes 🔥

This week I read H[appy Place](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/61718053-happy-place) by Emily Henry, [The Steel Kiss](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34030416-the-steel-kiss) by Jeffrey Deaver, and [Novel Love Story](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/199261152-a-novel-love-story) by Ashley Poston! They were all SO good! Emily Henry was one of my favorite writers from the moment I picked up her book [Beach Read](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/52867387-beach-read). She makes me laugh more than any other writer! Jeffrey Deaver is currently my favorite mystery author. He keeps my rapt attention and I'm always dying to know what happens next. Somehow I NEVER spot his twists coming, he really phrases things just right so I misunderstand and am looking the other way! Ashley Poston is quickly rising in my romance category and I've already placed a third book of hers on hold. I'm getting much better about staggering my requests online so I don't have 11 come in all at once (NEVER again!!) I am always dying for more library cards (more libraries mean more options and less wait time!) My biggest fear is running out of things to read whether its because of access or interest. I've gotten five "skip the line" books offered on Libby since we left the US!

This week we ended up finding TWO delicious spots for empanadas! One was at a restaurant called "La Imperfecta" which is only funny because I pointed out that restaurant strictly for the name on our second week here!
