Today was a day for exploration! We wanted to see the San Telmo Market which was offered on Sundays exclusively and realized it was very close to the ecological reserve — let's do both! We decided we would walk the reserve before heading to the market and making it back home before football started that afternoon 🐦🏪🏈

The reserve is massive and has few entrances so we had to walk a ways to reach the closest entrance.
