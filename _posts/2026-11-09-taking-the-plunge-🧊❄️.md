This morning we rolled into Andvord bay — a location that was blocked from us just days earlier. There is A LOT of ice around here and we aren't able to access any of the landing points. That meant we could do a zodiac cruise this morning instead!

Before that, I woke up early to get an icy workout in and held onto the railing while I worked out my legs. There were whales left and right! I couldn't believe there were pairs of whales on both sides of the boat! Naturally, they were there when I didn't have my camera 😑

I came back on deck after my workout, shower and breakfast and, of course, they were gone. I got some cool bird photos though!

That's right, Death in Antarctica...💀☠️🪦⚰️

We sifted through the evidence, combing through reports, logs, witness statements, call data, surveillance photos, medical statements, the medical examiner's report and suddenly people started running for the windows and doors!

A WHALE!!!!

I sprinted outside with my camera and waited...

and waited...

and waited...

Whale gone. DANG IT. 

It was then that Pippa made an announcement, we would be doing the Polar Plunge starting in less than TWENTY MINUTES!!! 

We had no idea that was coming and it hit us like a freight train 🚂 We were told ahead of time that the plunge with Oceanwide would only happen from the shore for safety purposes and we would have to walk into the water (like sadists) rather than jump from the boat and submerge all at once 🥽 Things had apparently changed 🪸

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795191302/s2k8mi26p555trlmkmbk.jpg)

Here we are, nervous as all get out and in our suits. I didn't want to get too cold so I kept my sweatshirt on till we had to leave the cabin. We set our heater on the highest setting and filled Mike's mug with piping hot water. We got two mugs ready with tea bags so we could have a hot drink to help warm us after. 

They called us by deck so deck three went first. The waiting felt like eternity even though there weren't too many who took the plunge from that deck. Deck four is massive and had the most plungers. They called the front half first (not us) but some went over early so we were in the back half of deck four, going near last from our group.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795191808/m3r5w4kiigistplyyj7r.heic)

Pre-dive! We couldn't dive together but they let us watch each other dive which was so sweet and really fun to support each other that way! Elizabeth, Jeannette, and Peggy were all watching, photographing, and videoing us in support 💗👏🏻📢

We did rock, paper, scissors to see who would go first and Mike won so he'd be going first. He would sprint to the shower after and start thawing and I would run in after him. As we lined up in the open doorway, it was FREEZING 🥶 and I did some squats to keep warm. A girl finished hers and said it really wasn't cold, just go fast. Yeah, ok crazy lady. But then Elizabeth came to wish us luck and said it wasn't bad either! 

Steffi, one of the guides said, it's not bad and put a towel around me as I walked out. 

> I said, "Steffi, you are German, nothing can hurt you."
>
> She threw her head back laughing and said "I like that!"

It was finally time, Mike headed for the stairs and Pippa was there, running the plunge. She directed him to the bottom of the stairs, where Gonzo would rope us up and told us to smile for Martin, behind the camera! He was all business as he descended the stairs.
