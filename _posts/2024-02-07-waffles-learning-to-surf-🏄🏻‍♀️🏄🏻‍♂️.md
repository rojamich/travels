This morning we woke up jittery with excitement! We get to check off another bucket list item ✅

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790884766/regivw5ve6oppsuuiu1g.jpg)

The sun was rising, turning the sky into beautiful purple and pinks as we planned out food for the day 🧇🥪

We decided to go have waffles on the beach! What life is this???

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790884773/wr7biyl9cyrynjpapai1.jpg)

Mine had apples and caramel on it 🤤while Mike had walnuts, powdered sugar and maple syrup! They were utterly delightful!

I'm glad we fortified because we would be spending hours with an instructor, learning how to surf! We walked over to the surf school where we met our photographer that day who would capture this amazing experience 📸

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790885777/frinksmwv6grjczws2lg.jpg)

Both our photographer and instructor were so kind and personable, it made me way less stressed about potentially being awful at surfing!

We got right to work on the warm sand, learning the proper way to stand up on the board from a lying position.

I kept assuming you need to squat much lower than necessary 😂

Our instructor felt we were ready to hit the water so we drug our boards out there, surfer style 🏄🏻🏄🏻‍♀️

He gave us more instructions in the water so we knew when to "catch" the wave. When to paddle, when to brace, and when to bound up. We also learned how to use our weight to stay upright.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790886792/hp6sz2cdywbn5k1lw2ec.jpg)

Per the usual, Mike was an instant natural. If you can't tell, I completely envy how athletic he is!
