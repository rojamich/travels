Wow, week two is in the books here in Buenos Aires and its an absolute blast — not to mention, we are loving the weather!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790190871/ah89zbpjfsszlsuvwq4m.jpg)

All days below 26.7°C (80f) and above 4.4°C (40f) are great!

It was even warm enough to spend time in the local parks, reading books and eating churros!

This was much better than the steak. I'm glad we tried them but I wouldn't order again. We will try another steakhouse just to make sure it wasn't a fluke!

We tried more empanadas too!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790191144/w0xclpjyd303eo7iktll.jpg)

And had new flavors, I tried one that was filled with cheese, tomato sauce, and basil — like a pizza! But our favorite is still the ground beef ones. 

We found a giant grocery store nearby where we needed an escalator to get to the second floor.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790191351/hbtnckwuo7uxugkwcncu.jpg)

And we were amused by how they hang the meats for sale!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790191349/wprfzuzkgpoyng3smai5.jpg)

There's a market nearby on Saturdays that we checked out and found spices, chocolate-covered raisins for Mike and a new sweater/shirt for me!

The weather held up long enough for us to read out on the balcony too 😍

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790191519/ify4pd28xdzooujdmpir.jpg)

I finished [Dolly All the Time](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/240687665-dolly-all-the-time) by Annabel Monaghan and LOVED IT! Mike is reading Jurassic Park with me and also listening to [Nomad](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/28954858-nomad) by James Swallow.

I really love how I can pick a park and safely run to it! I haven't felt unsafe at any time of day here and the routes are really beautiful.
