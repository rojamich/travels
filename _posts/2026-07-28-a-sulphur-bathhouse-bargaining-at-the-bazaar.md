Thank you and welcome back to another episode of "What the Heck are Mike & Jenna Up to Now?" 🤣🤣

It's 11pm here and I'm writing when I should be sleeping so... there's that. But! I did get in a beautiful run this morning and loved having the bridge and most of the city to myself while I pushed my limits! Trying to increase my mileage with every run and I'm really proud of myself - especially with the heat!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1785350903/20260729_065655_c1rwjs.jpg)

We scheduled the sulphur bathhouse appointment at the historic Chreli Abano bathhouse and were really excited to try out this new experience! We chose the N1 room if you're curious to check out the [website](http://www.chreli-abano.ge/)! It was a 30 minute walk from our place and public transportation would've been about the same so we said, let's do it!

Approaching the bathhouse, we were nervous about the Sulphur smell because it was slight outside so what would it be like inside?? 

Getting there early was grand and we were escorted straight to our spa room!

This room came with a hot tub, cold plunge, restroom, sauna, showers, and resting stone bed! Holy moly what more could you ask for?? It was already quite hot and humid in the room and we quickly stripped down to get in the shower to cool off. Then we remembered we need to get photos before it was too humid and got redressed 🤣

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1785351328%2F20260729_100744_skfvru.jpg%22%2C%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1785351332%2F20260729_100759_pf4hki.jpg%22%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22Us%20before%20the%20bathhouse%20experience!%22%7D %}

PSA: You do not want to bring jewelry or certain materials into the bathhouse because it can stain, rust, or get the Sulphur smell in the materials! We were very careful to avoid this!

After a cold shower, we dipped our toes into the hot tub. HOLY YIKES!!!🌵🌶️🔥🥵 They did not skimp on the boiling hot water (45°c or 113°f)! It took awhile to get used to and eventually we were able to submerge. We didn't last as long in there as we typically do with regular hot tubs and we jumped in to the FREEZING cold plunge 🥶❄️🌨️☃️My body is so confused but so refreshed! 

We opted in for the body scrub and Jenna's attendant came in first (why must I be the guinea pig for this??) She motions me to the stone bed and I lie face up. There's a rough scrub pad in her hand as she fills a pail with water. While the spigot is running water, I reach back to feel how hot or cold this is about to be to prepare myself 🤣 It's warm and she asks if that's good for me (how thoughtful!) Yes, and she gets to work scrubbing my top layer of skin off (and maybe more) like I'm a dirty floor in need of shine. By the time she's done scrubbing me, I feel like a newborn infant with brand new skin! It's really refreshing! 

She then fills a pillowcase with soap suds and gently lathers me up and it is the damnedest relaxed I've been in a long time. I'm talking near-drool relaxed. 

Mike's attendant comes in after mine and I soak in the hot tub while Mike gets a similar treatment. His attendant doesn't scrub him nearly as roughly (to Mike's regret) but does wash his hair (can you feel my envy from here?). 

I watch as the man leaves soap suds on Mike's face and I internally send him thoughts of calm as I know he hates getting soap in his eyes: stay strong honey 🧼🫧!!

For the next 45 minutes, we alternate from hot tub to cold plunge (lets be real, I'm mostly in the cold plunge) and Mike tries the sauna. He immediately looks like he's on fire and I'm well aware of my own limits and decide to skip it lol.

This was the perfect call for today and I'm so glad to do this after a run to recover!

We were on the hunt for better photos of the bazaar and some house shoes (we had to bring our disposable ones to the bathhouse and it was time for new ones).

The second stall we saw with sandals had exactly what we were looking for! Mike bargained with the owner for two pairs of shoes and I'm loving mine! They're exactly what I wanted (squishy, comfy, and cute!) Mike says his are good and remind him of Vietnam - dammit we miss that country so much 🥺)
