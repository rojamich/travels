Thank you and welcome back to another episode of "What the Heck are Mike & Jenna Up to Now?" 🤣🤣

It's 11pm here and I'm writing when I should be sleeping so... there's that. But! I did get in a beautiful run this morning and loved having the bridge and most of the city to myself while I pushed my limits! Trying to increase my mileage with every run and I'm really proud of myself - especially with the heat!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1785350903/20260729_065655_c1rwjs.jpg)

We scheduled the sulphur bathhouse appointment at the historic Chreli Abano bathhouse and were really excited to try out this new experience! We chose the N1 room if you're curious to check out the [website](http://www.chreli-abano.ge/)! It was a 30 minute walk from our place and public transportation would've been about the same so we said, let's do it!

Approaching the bathhouse, we were nervous about the Sulphur smell because it was slight outside so what would it be like inside?? 

Getting there early was grand and we were escorted straight to our spa room!

This room came with a hot tub, cold plunge, restroom, sauna, showers, and resting stone bed! Holy moly what more could you ask for?? It was already quite hot and humid in the room and we quickly stripped down to get in the shower to cool off. Then we remembered we need to get photos before it was too humid and got redressed 🤣

PSA: You do not want to bring jewelry or certain materials into the bathhouse because it can stain, rust, or get the Sulphur smell in the materials! We were very careful to avoid this!

After a cold shower, we dipped our toes into the hot tub. HOLY YIKES!!!🌵🌶️🔥🥵 They did not skimp on the boiling hot water! It took awhile to get used to and eventually we were able to submerge. We didn't last as long in there as we typically do with regular hot tubs
