Well, we try to snowshoe at least. There was plenty of snow! But it was hardpacked earth so we didn't actually need the snowshoes, dangit! I love snowshoeing. We enjoyed a slow morning with a delicious breakfast and coffee. When it finally started warming up outside we headed out to a hike nearby to try and snowshoe.

We weren't entirely sure where the hike started because there wasn't a sign but we watched a couple of dogs sprint over this hill so we thought it was worth a shot. It was steep!

There were lots of impressions in the snow, some looked like a moose or two came by!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2248%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1790093292%2Frqzgnk47aufmiyibzr9p.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1790093292%2Fs6hs6rz6hhcdq65pva23.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2296%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1790093296%2Fiftapqnltgyvcgwcc0pg.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

We were swept away by the beauty of it all. The pristine white and gorgeous, contrasting green!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790093347/amuc4wiwbkek76cge7jq.jpg)

We were careful to obey the yielding rules because several dogsledding teams raced by — out training!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790093047/jdh5rju0ix0z54syumgg.jpg)

At many spots it was narrow, but sometimes it opened up really wide!

We spotted animal-shapes in the snow formations and it felt like staring up at the clouds... look! It's a moose!

Climbing back up was a beast in itself 💪🏻

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790093516/nqfvv3ilcycapels0zwa.jpg)

That night, we soaked up the last of the fireplace, packed up, and cleaned like we were never there. It was hard to say goodbye to such a literal wild trip!
