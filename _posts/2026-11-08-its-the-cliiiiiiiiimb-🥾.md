That afternoon, our group was called to come to the lecture room to gear up for our mountaineering activity! We were really looking forward to a good workout and adventure! 

We were handed harnesses and told to put it on over all our clothing... I run very toasty, especially while exercising. Mike also gets warm while exercising so we put the harnesses on over everything but the parkas. During the initial briefing at the beginning of the cruise, they said this was a good idea but today, the guides were like, uh this should be OVER your parkas. We weren't given time to explain ourselves before we were ushered out the door to board the zodiacs. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795125242/iswhsg3csvzlsvwxrhyu.jpg "Armed with our icepicks! ")

Once on land, we stripped off our huge coats and were roped (through our harnesses) to our other mountaineering mates. We were a little uneasy because, at the beginning of the cruise, we were asked to rate our fitness level so we'd be sorted into groups that match fitness level and everyone gets the experience they should. Apparently (according to a guide), almost everyone puts intermediate (this is grossly inaccurate). Ranking ourselves a 7, we were in a group with the elderly who needed assistance in and out of the zodiacs (completely fine, of course! But we were really hoping for a good challenge).

We moved painfully slow. Our guide was actually the slowest person and I wonder now if it was because he pretty much mountaineered everyday for over a week...

At least the views were pretty, will we outrun the storm today??

We stopped at the top of the mountain while the guides talked... not sure what was happening, we peered around and enjoyed chatting with other groups and taking photos.

Our guide offered to take one and he looked great LOL

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795125624/cz2ewmm4drctb6ckvsna.jpg)

The snow was fresh and this spot was chosen for its lack of wildlife so I decided to enjoy some delicious snow.. it was SO good. I miss snow cones. A few people saw me and quizzically asked.. is it good? I said UM YES. And that's how I started a snow-eating trend and Mike laughed as he watched our mountaineer-mates scoop up snow and munch away.

Suddenly, I spotted our friend Julie grinning like mad! Tim was offering her something and she put her hands in her face while emphatically nodding yes... I waited a minute and asked... DID YOU GUYS JUST GET ENGAGED??? They had!! How incredible! He proposed on top of a mountain, in Antarctica, with a tiny penguin figurine 🥹

We noticed three zodiacs below us converging near a spot and later found out they saw orca whales!! This was a massive deal and the guides said they'd only seen orcas from the zodiacs one other time 😮 I was so sad we missed it for what turned out to be an ok hike.

The view down was pretty and we left in the nick of time! That storm caught up with us and we found ourselves in a whiteout AGAIN when we were back on the boat!
