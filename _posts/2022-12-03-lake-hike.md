We woke up with the sun and I headed through the hilly neighborhood to run around the lake. I was stopped twice by two wonderfully friendly pups for some pets and kisses! What a great start to the day! The lake had a narrow dirt path that led me down to the shore and blessed me with this view!

[![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785680454/blogger-import/06529f64d02fab3453fb.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/v1785680463/blogger-import/0d455a0e934dcc34d10c.jpg)

  


We were lucky to have sunny weather this day for our lake hike and some beautiful scenery! We hiked the cove where I got to pet ANOTHER DOG!!!
