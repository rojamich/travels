2. People who never leave their country will be the ones telling you other countries are unsafe. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1785262524/20240829_155212_mw5bgr.jpg)

I cannot tell you the number of people who have warned us against countries - everything from Greece to South Africa was too dangerous to explore. Are there dangerous places out there? Absolutely. That's why we do research, take precautions, and are very careful. We are still susceptible to harm of course. But that's no reason to lock yourself away at home. In fact, the only time Mike and I were in serious, life-threatening danger, was in the US when a man chased us with a knife in a San Diego park.

Speaking of home, the US doesn't typically rank higher than #50 in the safest countries lists.
