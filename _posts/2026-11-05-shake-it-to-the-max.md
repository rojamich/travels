If you were wondering, here is the defined difference between an iceberg and a glacier:

> "A glacier is a massive, permanent body of compressed snow and ice that forms on land and flows slowly under its own weight, while an iceberg is a floating chunk of ice that has broken off (calved) a glacier or ice shelf and drifts in the ocean."

We dragged ourselves to each meeting and immediately went back to sleep in between.  if you don’t attend the briefing, you can’t participate in the activity later!

I survived on crackers, ginger ale, and a protein bar today, but some came back to visit me :(
