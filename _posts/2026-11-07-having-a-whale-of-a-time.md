We weren’t booked for any excursions today (kayaking, mountaineering, or camping), so that means we were set for a zodiac landing on the island of Cuverville in Antarctica! I decided to check out the scenery before breakfast and ended up getting photos of a whale!

**\[photos to be added asap!]**

After a hearty breakfast, we geared up and got in line to load into a zodiac that would take us to shore. The food has been good and plentiful! I’m relieved that it hasn’t been the average-to-bad normal cruise food. I have so many options for things I am willing to try!

While we were lined up, we were able to see another whale! It was incredible! 

**\[photos/videos to be added asap!]**


We pulled up to the rocky shoreline … where we were planning to do the polar plunge! Why am I doing this again?? A colony of swimming penguins greeted us as we arrived 🙂
**\[photos to be added asap!]**

The mountains were MASSIVE here! I’m not sure why I always pictured Antarctica as a flat ice mass 🤦🏻‍♀️

The gentoo penguins were so cute and clumsy, trying to get onto the icy land from the rocky beach!

It was a beautiful bay, and we were blown away by the icebergs and number of penguins here!

We had a nice surprise today when we were visited by an Adeli penguin who absolutely runs the beach 😂

That's when a leopard seal showed up…
