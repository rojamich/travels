Utterly and completely smitten with Istanbul, Turkey is the perfect way to describe this brief love affair with this city 😍🕌

I was really nervous — what will Turkey be like? There were so many shared experiences (negative) that we weren't sure if we would like it. Thankfully, the hostility, boiling hot weather, and swaths of scams weren't at all what we were exposed to here!

Everyone was so kind, welcoming and thrilled over our attempts at Turkish:

* Hello - Merhaba
* Thank you - Teşekkür ederim
* Please - Lütfen

The food here was less intimidating, if I can try Armenian food, I can try Turkish food! We really enjoyed so many of the flavorful dishes and its been so rewarding to be exposed to more spices 🫚🧅🌿🧄. If you don't know, I love putting spices on everything. You should see our eggs 🍳. By the time I'm done cooking, you're looking for the egg in the breakfast 👀. 

Mike and I used the eSIM app: Redex here, which worked well everywhere we went! Wifi was also available in most restaurants and cafes, upon request. This was easier here to ask for since many menus were online so it was a given that we would want wifi 🛜. 

We used (a lot) of walking and uber here, that's it 🚶🏻‍♀️‍➡️🚶🏻‍♂️‍➡️!

If we replayed the week, we would spend more time in the Karaköy neighborhood and probably skip the Kadıköy neighborhood, only because there’s so much to see in Karaköy and only so much time on our first go-round. I ate a lot of baklava here but I would eat even more. There's nothing like it anywhere else and I miss it already (its been 2 hours ⏱️).

I learned that there are more than just two major mosques here and its like everywhere you turn is another massive but phenomenal mosque. I don't think I would tire of running along the sea and admiring their minarets 🕌. 

The water here is avoided thanks to old plumbing and we were excited that our hotel sprovided free bottled water! Yay 🫗!

**Daily weather**: Low 80°Fs- low 90s (26°C to 34°C) during the day, mid 60s (15.6°C) at night.
