Traveling is still relatively new for us (two years ago we had our first overseas trip!) and I had no idea how I'd feel in a new environment — turns out, it was pretty awesome and I have a budding obsession with travel ✈️🚂🚢

Mike and I used a eSIM app here, which worked well everywhere we went! Wifi was largely unavailable outside of our VRBO. Working cafes and gathering places were not popular here as we were staying in a highly touristy town.  We originally planned this trip as a couple's trip with my parents so we stayed in Tamarindo but our next trip will probably span the country so we can see a lot more!

We used walking and organized transfers for our modes of transportation here 🚶🏻‍♀️🚶🏻‍♂️🚗!

Some thoughts for Costa Rica:

We probably could’ve spent at least a month enjoying and exploring this country! It was mid-Winter term for my teaching so a large-scale travel wasn't available and we were restricted to traveling broadly to summers and one month in Winter. With more time, we would love to go all over the country! The roads aren't easy to navigate (not smooth or marked well) so one week was not doable (without A LOT of stress lol).

Total distance walked: 44.6 miles

That’s like walking from Sacramento, CA to Fairfield, CA  ☀️! 

OR

Savannah, GA to Hinesville, GA🐊!

OR

Portland, OR to Salem, OR! 🌲

Thank you for coming along with us 😃
