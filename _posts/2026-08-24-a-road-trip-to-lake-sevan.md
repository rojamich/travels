We woke up bright-eyed and bushy-tailed, ready to hit the gym before our road trip up to Lake Sevan! The lake isn't too far from us so it was nice to make time for a workout and to make breakfast!

It was a challenging but great workout (two days later and my forearms and hamstrings are still sore!). Mike surprised me by making time for us to grab a cà phê muối! It was delicious and a great way to start the drive ☕🚗

We listened to music and a podcast on the way up, mostly freeway with some interesting views!

Lake Sevan is a hot tourist destination and we were lucky to get free, shaded parking! It was a short walk up a hill, past stalls selling souvenirs of all sorts!

We saw this cute little eclectic yard that reminded me of one I'd see on the California coast!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787823271/20260825_125218_vuoqgs.jpg)

There were a few sets of stone steps to ascend the hill overlooking Lake Sevan — the largest body of water in Armenia! And look how beautiful and blue it is! Definitely reminds us of Lake Como!

Atop the hill sits the 9th-century monastery of Sevanavank!

It really was a lovely view with a good breeze. Thankfully it was only in the 70s here and we were able to walk along the hill to catch each viewpoint 🌄

We decided to try an Armenian food spot nearby and Mike got the Sig (whitefish) and cherry compote drink and I ordered the chicken kebab. They were both average and disappointing.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787821820/20260825_135130_xrodwj.jpg)

The restaurant was really beautifully decorated though!
