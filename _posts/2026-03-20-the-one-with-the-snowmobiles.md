For all of those at home keeping track, the Alaska bucket list is as follows:

✅Dog Sledding

✅Northern Lights

⏹️See a Moose

⏹️Snow Mobile

I couldn't believe we had had such luck with the list so far! It can be really hard to see the Northern Lights sometimes and we nailed it on the first try! 

This morning we were headed to snowmobile — that's right! As long as the weather holds up, we will hit another bucket list item. Mike had looked up tips for spotting Meese (that's not the real plural for moose but it is to me. It should be just like Goose and Geese.) One tip was to look on the edges of woods just past the tree line. 

We were heading along the freeway when I suddenly shouted MOOOOOOOOSE!!!!! Mike flipped a fast U-turn and the moose (in all its glory) was chomping away in the trees, just where Mike said it would be!

Wrap it up and call it a day, folks! Cuz it does NOT get better than this! We were pretty high the rest of the day after having spotted that moose. It was the second most difficult thing to accomplish on the list and I just can't believe we nailed it. Great job moose!

We made it to the snowmobile shop and it was empty so we waited a bit — the instructions said to be there early for gear or you will be left 😱

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790011576/y7qkpxv8qzcenoukdual.jpg)

I used their beaver mitts (named for the fur) because I had gotten mild frostbite two days earlier while dogsledding 🤕 The gentleman told us the glove name and I stared at him blankly and asked if it was because we were from Oregon (home of the Beavers). He thought that was hilarious 😂

We headed out to get our bikes and he tried putting me behind Mike! Can you believe that? Like I wouldn't want to drive my own??

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2259%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1790011738%2Ffd0ywv6ljaitx6gtchre.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22SO%20excited!%20And%20yes%2C%20those%20are%20my%20motorcycle%20pants%20keeping%20me%20very%20warm%20lol.%22%7D %}

At the final hour, a couple of girls joined in on the adventure!

The five of us set out and were pulling out of the lot when a man and his son arrived (the last two of our party) and we didn't stop for them... I was surprised, they actually missed their riding window 😱

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1790011864/rvjhcode9vekkh9uqykg.jpg "Here we are, pulling out of the lot! Don't worry, that truck is parked lol")

We followed the guide through the winding trails and eventually to a wide area with a slope where we could ride up and down it like a half-pipe or like 4-wheeling! It was so exhilarating.
