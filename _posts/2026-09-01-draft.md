Great weather was predicted today — no higher than 85! Time to celebrate with a run 🏃🏻‍♀️‍➡️ Jogging downstairs, I reached the bottom floor door to the lobby and... nothing. Door didn't budget. I heard feet shuffle and the door *clunked* open. A sleepy-eyed hotel employee greeted me, half-awake and followed me to the front door to open that too. I quickly made a mental note that we would have to go out the third-story window should their be a fire, and set off for my run!

I made it to Moda Pier and soaked up the park views of the sea.

That morning we decided to try traditional Turkish breakfast food and found a really cute restaurant with ultra-friendly staff! On the walk over, we noticed a business had tables filled with chess!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788457296/gb7hbwhqgtmkxzjvrdmf.jpg)

Mike got black tea and I tried the homemade lemonade 🍋

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788457429/uhgd3cgqsz3pzce5mu8c.jpg)

We ordered (left) Sucuklu Yumurta - fried eggs and sausage and (right) Menemen - scrambled eggs with tomato, peppers, and spices. They were both so flavorful and good! I prefer the Sucuklu Yumurta though. The bread was ultra-soft too.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788455594/cvm3rkyfyxd5pcmbbwv8.jpg)

We headed to a downright gorgeous coffeeshop around the corner and \*gasp\* they make an *amazing* pumpkin spice latte. If there were books in here I would be concerned that I died.

 It would be offensive in my culture not to try a pastry here so the cookie pie had to happen. It was just fabulous and the perfect writing snack ✍🏻🍪🥧

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788458492/asmejowd054tstkbhbsk.jpg)

That evening we tried more Turkish food likeL

* Girit Boregi - crispy fried pastry with meat and cheese
* Girit Ezme - crushed herb and vegetable spread
* Yaprak Ciğer - lamb liver

We really enjoyed the first two dishes 🫷🏻
