I was unbelievably excited to go to the post office today! How many people can truthfully say that?! The head of reception (Ingrid) had placed our likelihood of going at 50% 😮 Initially, she didn't want to give us an estimate of our likelihood. Mike "sweet-talked" her into it by saying that he would be disappointed no matter what 😂 The ship offered free post cards so we got to pick whichever ones we liked! They also had an "official" stamp that I LOVE and am totally adding to my travel tattoo!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795114593/uvuog6a08yxuyw5eoypa.jpg)

We lined up and boarded zodiacs for the short trip to shore. This was the sole morning site and was very small so we had to take turns. Surprisingly, we were in one of the first trips ashore!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795114644/x56qwf9apuh8bolqtzrc.jpg)

It was a small and short landing today. The post office was surrounded by penguins so we had entertainment while waiting to enter the post office and then waited for a zodiac.

**Port Lockroy's "Penguin Post Office"** is officially the world's southernmost public post office! In case that wasn't obvious 😉They have over 70,000 post cards flow through this office *every* year and deliver to over 100 countries! What is REALLY interesting is that it came about due to a secret WWII mission from Britain building a  base here for territorial claims and intel! It is now a post office and museum.
