We had a late flight into Singapore, arriving around 11pm! Normally, I expect to see the city lights on the way in, to our surprise, it was boats lit up on the water 😱🚤🛥️

Thankfully we had a short ride to our hotel because we were tired and it was TOASTY, daytime is (30°C to 31°C (86°F–88°F) and night doesn't really dip below 23°C to 24°C (73°F–75°F)! After a wild trip to Vietnam, we were tuckered out and I was so tempted to just stay in the hotel for the next two days! Mike talked some sense into me and, after a night of sleep, we got started on our itinerary! 

But first, food!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787042864/20260103_105340_xdghzc.jpg "The donuts were... ok. And SO expensive!")

I was just hoping wherever we went next would have shade!

We were able to walk through the marketplace that houses some interesting business names (a highlight for us when we travel through Asia!)

There were never-ending, incredible architecture that was so unique! I felt like my head was constantly on a swivel!

We made it to the Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple dedicated to the goddess and destroyer of evil. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787043392/20260103_114057_npkebm.jpg)

We removed our shoes at the entrance to show respect. It's be interesting to see the different ways to show respects in different religions! Some require skirts, others head coverings, some shoe removal and even shoulder-coverage! 

Check out how many distinctly different statues there are 🤯

We passed the Angullia Mosque that was built in the 1890s 🕌

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787043852/20260103_115306_duzvz3.jpg)

Not too far down the road we saw the Sakya Muni Buddha Gaya Temple aka Temple of 1,000 Lights!

Inside held an incredible sight!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787044333/20260103_120507_bg2rcl.jpg "This statue is 15 meters (or 50 feet) tall and weighs 300 tons!")

Holy moly! We felt so little!

It was lunch time so we stopped for some Indian food - it was good but the mango lassi was not.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787044731/20260103_124241_fc2gcw.jpg)

We headed to the Muslim Quarter of Singapore! It was so cool to see how this area of the city state completely transformed in architecture, religious sites, street art, and even the rooves were different!
