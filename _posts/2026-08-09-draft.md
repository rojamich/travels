Wow, its really time to leave our Airbnb 😱 We had a relaxing morning - I finished my book! [Red Rising](https://www.goodreads.com/series/117100-red-rising-saga) has been surprisingly awesome! Mike talked me into trying it as it's not my normal genre. I am really soaking it up!

Our driver for the day (Avtandil) picked us up at 9am and we started the difficult journey of leaving Tbilisi. Not because its emotionally taxing, but because many of the roads leading to the highway are under construction! We eventually made our way out of the city and hit the open road. It was not windy so that was great, he was a good driver too so no carsickness! I put my podcast on and rotated napping and listening.

There is a tragic tale about this river and how it came to be.

**The Tale:**

Two sisters, one blonde and one brunette, both fell in love with the same brave knight. The knight married the blonde sister.

Heartbroken and unwilling to ruin their happiness, the dark-haired sister jumped from a mountain into a gorge; her tears formed the Black Aragvi. Overwhelmed by grief and missing her sister, the blonde sister also leaped into the abyss; her tears formed the White Aragvi.

Overcome with remorse, the knight later perished from sorrow, turning into a third stream that joined them further down.

On that sad note, lets continue our journey to Stepantsminda.
