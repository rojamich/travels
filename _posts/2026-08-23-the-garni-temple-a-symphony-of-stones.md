The night before our daytrip up to Garni, we got a message from Hertz rental reminding us of our reservation. Once we confirmed he said... one question... we waited anxiously. He said, are you already in the city (Yerevan)? We said yes and he offered for us to pick up our rental in downtown Yerevan! What a fantastic surprise! Otherwise, we would have to get a GG (uber) to the airport and drive all the way back and *then* to Garni! Its about 45 minutes to the airport alone!

We would've booked our rental there to start with but it was about 20x more expensive than the airport 🤕So this was a crazy surprise! They also were able to change the reservation to later this morning so now I had time to run before we went!

Here is our rental car! How cute!

We decided to walk to the rental since it was only 27 minutes away and we somehow turned that into a 47 minute walk (yay for burning calories??) but we finally found the rental location! We were able to leave the car there and head to a coffee shop around the corner for breakfast and coffee - wow, what an awesome start to the day 🌄☕🥳

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1787593605/20260824_104218_fg7jjr.jpg)

On the road, it was a bit wild getting into the city. I am so utterly impressed by Mike's composure driving in different countries. I would be so stressed and overwhelmed. He went with the flow and learned on the go! (That rhymed). 

Our first stop was at the Garni Temple which is a 1st century temple, influenced by Greek/Roman design! 

We entered through a stone archway and immediately walked to the first sight, The Greek Inscription of Garni.
