This was a very short exploration of a country that's the same size as New York City! We fully expect to revisit it multiple times as it is a common layover stop across the world! We actually chose to visit Singapore because it was a free stopover from Vietnam to LAX - this means it wouldn't cost us an extra flight if we sandwiched a few days here between Vietnam and home 🤯 Who could pass up that deal??

It stays around 80s year-round here and is very humid. If you know how I feel about heat and humidity, you know it was hard for me to give this country a fair shot at being a favorite. I turn into a real cranky McCrankerton when I'm too hot and have no way of cooling off. The food was also a letdown here but lets hope the next trip improves things with more time to explore! I don't think it'll get any cheaper though 😥

Despite the heat, the architecture was unique and really impressive — a real highlight of this stop! 

Mike and I used the eSIM app here, which worked well everywhere we went! There wasn't wifi anywhere in public and I feel like this country would've fared better if we hadn't just visited the most incredible country we had ever seen!

We only used metro, grab, and lots of walking as transportation!

Total miles walked in 4 days of Singapore: 33.71 miles. Yep, roughly 8 miles a day 😂🚶🏻‍➡️🚶🏻‍♀️‍➡️👣

That’s like walking from Savannah, GA to Hilton Head Island, SC! 🏝️
