This morning was a relaxed one, and thankfully so because we stayed up watching football the night before! Our flight wasn't until this evening so we had time to get ready, pack, and get to a coffee shop to work. 

I got a lot of grading done and some of the blog worked on which was *really* nice before a lot of traveling to our next continent 🗺️✈️

Our uber drove like he was late for work but thankfully it was a short ride to the airport! We had time to relax in the lounge as well, here in Ushuaia — it was so surprising that they had one! It's such a tiny airport! Understandably, it was tiny. Mike somehow found us seats together and we were able to get back to work 📝

We had a 3.5 hour flight and one row apart, in aisle seats. It was a flight full of reading! I saw SO many people reading kindles, including all three of us in my row! I got to chatting with them, they're from North Carolina and had come down to hike Patagonia! I got to finish my book: [Part of Your World by Abby Jimenez](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/58684524-part-of-your-world) which was amazing and she's one of my favorite authors!

We arrived in Buenos Aires around 830pm and had the worst dinner at outback steakhouse. When will I realize that the meals in the US are not the same overseas?? 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795454757/mh93cgz5rusiupqcuhlc.jpg)

The fries were a redeeming quality, super-seasoned and soft!

We caught the free shuttle to the hotel only 15 minutes away. We didn't have to go all the way into BA for the night. It was a quick turnaround but at least we finally had a big bed after sleeping in twin beds for a few weeks! The air conditioner was wonderful too 🪭

It was hard to drag ourselves out of bed this morning but we managed it! Packed up again, we headed to the shell gas station next door for plane snacks! We grabbed the free shuttle back to the airport to fly to Madrid, Spain! The airport lounge was HUGE. Massive food bar, shower, and tons of comfy chairs. Too bad we didn't have longer to enjoy it! There was also a very loud family in there 😔

Coffee in tow, we were off to board ⏰ After handing over our passports, we were asked for our return (home) flights as proof we would be leaving the country... Um we don't have flights.. We are taking a train to the south of Spain and then Morocco by ferry... We tried asking if they wanted airbnb reservations and they ended up settling for our flight from Morocco to Georgia (whew 😅)

Completely spoiled, we were in for another lie-flat flight!

Less than thrilled that the loud family was behind me... but that's what the noise-cancelling headphones are for, right??

The stewardesses immediately served beverages and we perused the menu for dinner 🦐🍝

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795455622%2Fqoooexesnixxjb2yiaaz.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795455621%2Fzcv0qerbrvqf4vqfqt4s.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1795455612%2Fqin4fjnqhcpmdi5meihb.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Mike got the braised chuck tender with potatoes, peppers, and bacon. I got the pumpkin & goat cheese pasta and we both received poached salmon (I quickly shuffled mine off to Mike as I'm only team salmon when it's in sushi lol). The cheese board was really good though and we know this because I ate it too fast to photograph it.

I ended up taking a long nap but couldn't sleep for a long time after that! It was a 12 hour flight but took off around 1pm. We were really hoping to sleep, hydrate, and eat well to avoid jetlag.

Well... we had hydrated ✅, ate well ✅... where's my sleep?!

Mike enjoyed the new spiderman movie on the flight and was reading [The Dungeon Anarchist's Cookbook ](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/211721809-the-dungeon-anarchist-s-cookbook)by Matt Dinniman

I was reading [Dead Mountain](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/17557470-dead-mountain) by Donnie Eichar which was a fascinating book on Russian hikers that died mysteriously. When I still couldn't sleep, I read [Yours Truly](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/61918816-yours-truly) (the next book in the series) by Abby Jimenez. Also, AMAZING book. I gobbled it up!

Before we knew it, we were landing in Madrid. Could we take another lap, Mr. Pilot? Mr. Pilot says no. We shuffled off the plane and onto an airport bus to get to the terminal where we took approximately 30 seconds to get through a facial recognition passport control 🤯 It was amazing. 

This airport is MASSIVE and the bus between terminal 1- Terminal 4 takes ten minutes! That's insane for an airport distance! It was less amazing that we landed at 5am local time and the airport lounge was booked solid til 1030am. 😩 Worse still, our hotel didn't have early check-in and we couldn't tack on another night for cost because they were fully booked. Filled with jetlag and frustration, Mike got us onto the airport train that led us into the city. We then transferred to another another train that would lead us to our hotel. 

Three different sets of locals helped us with doors at the trains. Some of them take tap on your phone, some you just walk through, and others needed a preloaded special card. Without asking, they saw us confused and immediately jumped in to help. Holding the hand of his mother, a little boy saw us and declared from 10 meters away, "Just walk through!", in Spanish! It was the cutest little thing 🥹

Successfully through all the transportation types ✈️🚏🚉🚃 we stepped out into the square.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795456793/zydoju2o0hmmq4vyqxfl.jpg)

It's officially Christmas season over here! Pretty cool to see the tree being constructed 🌲🎄Our hotel offered to let us store our bags while we sought nourishment so we ditched our big ones and kept our small laptop bags to find a spot to work. 

Like a beacon of hope, Starbucks was a two minute walk from our hotel and OPEN 👐🏻☕😮‍💨

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1795456809/nn1coqwnq3qldgmsv92v.jpg)

At 8am, the rest of the city was absolutely still asleep. We worked and replenished ourselves happily in the warmth. Its actually pretty chilly here 10°c (50s f) and overcast. Mike unintentionally slipped into a nap and woke up 10 minutes later shocked with himself 😅

Around noon, we packed up and hauled ourselves over to the taco bell 🔔 a few doors down. It was quite terrible. One of the biggest regret meals so far on this journey. Nothing like US TB so I still haven't learned. We walked over to our hotel and read books in the lobby until our room was ready (thankfully super early). We showered and crashed for a couple hours to catch up on rest 💤 But hey, we are in SPAIN! 😍
