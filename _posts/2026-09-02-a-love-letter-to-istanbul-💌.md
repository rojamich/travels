A little heartbroken this morning knowing we will be saying goodbye to Istanbul in a few short hours 🥺 I decided to dance while I watched the sunrise 💃🏻

We grabbed pastries and the delicious coffee at our coffee shop again before heading to the airport. I finished by book: [Beach Read](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/52867387-beach-read) by Emily Henry and started working on A Textbook Case by Jeffery Deaver - it's #9.5 in the series!

The ride to the airport was an easy one, we scheduled a pickup with Black Lane (a service offered by one of our cards) and it was a very comfy ride with ice cold air conditioning!  Can you believe this is the view coming into the airport?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788527724/pvli6ab9o8etd6ghaos6.jpg)

We booked a business class flight to get to Toronto so we had access to the business lounge! Even better, we were able to reserve a conference room all to ourselves 🤯🤯🤯WHAT??!!
