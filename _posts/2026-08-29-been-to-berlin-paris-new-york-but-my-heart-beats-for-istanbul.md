This was not a night for a lot of sleep, we were too excited to see Istanbul today! Plus, we couldn't arrange for an gg the night before so we had to wake up early and hope to find a driver that morning! We packed all up and headed down to an easier pick up point for the gg and watched the sun rise!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788196774/20260830_060111_ubiwb7.jpg)

Mike finally got the chance to try a vending machine coffee and said it was good!

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1788197011%2F20260830_055722_yjwirn.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1788196944%2F20260830_055838_n1vf9j.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1788196932%2F20260830_055844_w3fp2a.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

It was adorable how it came out! The cup dropped down and was held in place while the espresso and water poured in!

We had an easy ride to the airport and were able to enjoy the lounge – as a bonus, we had just enough cash for a mini cinnamon roll at the airport, it was delicious 🤤

We boarded a bus for the flight and it let us all out in a hodge podge in front of the plane. How unorganized!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788197331/20260830_090247_kgrwg5.jpg)

It was an easy, 2.5 hour flight to Istanbul from Yerevan (hooray 🥳)! We only had one minor hiccup on the flight and that's when *someone* spilt *a lot* of cold water on themselves –pants and sweatshirt sleeve were soaked! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788197579/20260830_092815_qp8bdp.jpg "P.s. It was totally me.")

The airport was massive, clean, and not packed full of people which was a nice change from other major airports. I did get confused with the restrooms though...

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788197818/20260830_113222_ica8md.jpg)

I thought this sign was indicating the women's bathroom but she's actually just pointing the restroom! 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788197819/20260830_113226_hvpmnp.jpg)

This is a bathroom for pets lol. Thankfully, I didn't get too far before realizing this!

The Ubers work a little differently here. They give you an estimate on the ride cost but you still get hailed a taxi. That's at least a little more secure than just calling a taxi but I still prefer a straight up uber. The views on the way in from the airport blew our minds! We went through a gorgeous green forest and emerged into city with insane architecture! 

{% gallery_block %7B%22photos%22%3A%5B%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1788198352%2F20260830_120930_ul5qb0.jpg%22%7D%2C%7B%22x%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22y%22%3A%2250%22%2C%22image%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fres.cloudinary.com%2Fdgw35sldo%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%2Cw_1200%2Fv1788198414%2F20260830_121223_0_tqlosr.jpg%22%7D%5D%2C%22caption%22%3A%22%22%7D %}

Our hotel was staffed by such a welcoming man who immediately gave us cold water! Thankfully it was not hot outside and we definitely were it expecting it to be broiling out! It was really weird to be back in traveling mode since we had stayed in Tbilisi for a month and then Yerevan for two weeks. We would only be at this hotel for two nights and then onto another for two more nights!

We headed out to see the sights, naturally, we needed reinforcements. For energy of course!

But, it wouldn't be a Michael & Jenna adventure without some hill walking. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788199799/20260830_164926_y8bppe.jpg)

This was just one of many hills we walked on a day of 23k steps!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788198639/20260830_170309_bv8kcr.jpg "Mike and I got the same flavors! Nutella for one scoop and gingerbread for the other - they were so effing good!")

Plus, we got to enjoy them while admiring the Galata Tower!

Time to flâner towards the major city sites! We turned the corner and this very Turkish car pulls around blasting the song "Apple bottom jeans, boots with the fur!" Mike and I just tried not to openly stare lol

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788200028/20260830_170957_csucvc.jpg)

Does this window look like a spiderweb to you too?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788200279/20260830_171451_ik7avv.jpg)

Today was Turkish Victory day! We didn't even plan it this way!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788200647/20260830_171542_itj5lm.jpg "This is the celebration of the victory at the Battle of Dumlupınar in 1922.")

The streets were lined with flags everywhere 🚩

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788200736/20260830_171724_dvfvsx.jpg)

This might be the most picturesque city I've ever seen in my life. I think I'm in love!

We said, wait, so is this the Blue Mosque or Hagia Sophia? Turns out, neither of these mosques were either of those options – that's how many mosques they have here!

At this very moment, a massive flock of birds took off!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788202287/IMG_0073_ero9ay.jpg)

He has become quite the bird nerd.

The first is the Süleymaniye Mosque and the second is the Yeni Cami Mosque. I absolutely love these views! I am seriously obsessed with this cityscape. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788200947/20260830_173026_bfvoay.jpg "We are just so grateful to be here!")

We crossed over the bridge and it was filled with people which made sense once we remembered Istanbul ranks among the top 10 most-touristed cities on Earth 🌍👯🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Wouldn't you know it, we actually did end up finding Hagia Sophia!

It's currently undergoing renovations so we didn't get to see her in her full glory but her size is still really impressive!

The Blue Mosque was an incredible site to behold! It has it's own outer and inner square with enough room for picnicking and fountains!

I spotted a great depictor of this great city – there are so many cats and mosques here!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788202371/IMG_0110_prbwtr.jpg)

It was at this moment a little girl and her family approached me, the little girl dragging her grandmother by the hand. She pointed to my camera, asking for a photograph. I nodded, excited to help! They posed and she motioned to offer to pay me. I shook my head and offered to take their photos with their phone. They turned out so cute! Mike happened to catch the moment!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788202479/20260830_183030_a9cgsj.jpg)

We were allowed to enter the inner square for the Blue Mosque – a site we would visit the interior of tomorrow. This was a scouting mission!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1788202583/20260830_183603_ubzptf.jpg)

Noting the rules, where to get scarves, and the visitor entrance, we just took in the views and the details.
