Mike & I met in ROTC in August 2011 and that's all she wrote! Two months later we were dating, one month later he gave me a promise ring which I happily accepted!

In January of 2012, Mike suggested we go on our first trip together 🌴🐚🏖️🌊 Yes, absolutely, lets do it!

He planned it from A to Z and we drove down to Santa Maria!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1793366133/vp4uha5zzpw4rd8aqyzn.png "This was a postcard we saved over the years from that first trip!")

I was SO impressed by the fancy hotel (I've never stayed in one before!) It was cozy with a great view and we were near a beach. What more could I want there?! 

We took multiple trips down to the beach and strolled the pier — still a new couple but comfortable enough to be goofy together 😄

We saw our first movie together in theaters: The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo. If you've seen the movie, you know that's a pretty dark film for a first movie date lol! That's pretty on theme for Mike's family though. Mario took Laura to see a Bridge Too Far for their first movie date 🎥🎬🍿

Afterwards, we looked at each other and said, wow... that was... dark 😧

We both needed to stay on our a game for ROTC so we hiked there too. Turns out, being active together is still one of our favorite pastimes 💪🏻
