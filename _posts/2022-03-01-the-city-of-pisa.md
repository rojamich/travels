We went looking for a recommended pizza place in Pisa. Get it? Pizza in Pisa? We instead found this Pizza place where the other was supposed to be and gave it a try! It was pretty odd but delicious! Kinda like a calzone but not as much bread. The toppings are inside!

We only had time for one more stop and Mike was kind enough to oblige me! We went back to Filter coffee where I slowly sipped my delicious coffee once more before another few weeks of coffee-less existence. It was a relaxing stop here and the workers gave me free stickers! So grateful to have my caramel again.
