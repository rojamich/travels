Before embarking on this life-changing adventure, Mike and I sat in our home office and asked ourselves, what would you regret missing out on in life? What places or experiences are bucket lists for us?

Gorilla trekking? The Galapagos? Diving with whale sharks? Son Doong Cave? Seeing all seven continents? The Pyramids? The Great Wall? Hot Air ballooning over Cappadocia?

We ranked them based on our excitement, the cost, what physical agility was needed, and if we thought the site/experience was at risk of becoming impossible to complete/see. 

The ones we were most excited for:

1. Gorilla Trekking
2. Galapagos Islands
3. Antarctica

The ones we were most concerned would be at risk:

1. Antarctica 
2. Gorilla Trekking
3. The Great Wall

The greatest physical agility demand:

1. The Galapagos Islands
2. The Great Wall
3. Polar Bear Road Trip/Train Trip in Canada

The most costly:

1. Antarctica
2. The Pyramids
3. Swimming with Orca whales

Before watching [Allison Anderson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDap9qxb96k) travel to Antarctica a few years ago, we didn't realize this was a feasible trip people actually went on. Other than expeditioners and scientists, of course! We dismissed it as out of the realm of possibility and something we could wait on til retirement. We'd have more money saved up and the physical demand wouldn't be bad.

But now, after looking at our list, we knew we'd need to prioritize it. Mike reached out to the company to get an estimate and began working with an agent to make this dream become a reality! We were really glad we did too, because 1) there will probably always be another place to use that money on and if we didn't commit, would we ever? 2) The physical demand was actually more than we expected. Part of that might be the expedition-style we chose, making it more affordable but also adventurous! But there are some things that you can't get away from. The biggest one we saw was the difficulty getting on and off the ship. It wasn't an easy stroll (or wheelchair) down the gangway.
