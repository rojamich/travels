Rounding the corner of a nearby coffee shop, we turned to open the door and we hear, "HEY!" Having recognized zero people and rarely speaking more than the niceties to others, we were startled. It took me a full three seconds to register the person standing before us!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794042854/i49znsseaf4dh4tql8t2.jpg)

If you remember our trip to Vietnam, and how we explored the Ha Giang Loop, this person may be familiar to you!  We were in a motorcycle touring group together for three days! We could not believe she was standing before us on a completely different continent! She told us she's also going on a cruise... No... it couldn't be! OH YES!!! We are ON THE SAME CRUISE TOO!!! We have officially been to more countries/continents with her than anyone else 🫨😂 

She's cruising with her aunt and mom and we were excited that we would actually know someone on this voyage!

We found seats in the cafe and settled in for the wait to board. Mike enjoyed a ham and cheese, while I got the Cesar salad. They weren't bad! I finally got to eat bacon again 🥓

Four hours, one smoothie (Mike), and one milkshake (me) later, we were packing up to head to the pier. It was a super productive 4 hours of blogging and grading!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794042967/i6tvjituykjwdy6h8il6.jpg)

I forgot to mention that we walked past a bookstore downtown and, since we had time, we went in to see the English selection. I was thrilled to find “Murder on the Orient Express” by Agatha Christie! Its one of my favorite movies and I have never read the book! Stocking up on snacks and (now) books. It was time to board. 

It was a surprisingly quick boarding process with so few passengers (107) and we walked right on! The staff was warm, welcoming, and excited as it was the first expedition of the season!

We booked the cheapest room possible to afford this insanely expensive adventure and it actually has a lot more room than we thought it would!

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794068218/tvdsbt9b3pwjuofkahre.jpg)

Our rented gear was waiting for us in the cabin. Lugging around waterproof pants, a massive parka, multiple sets of gloves and long socks were not feasible for the last four months! We also got this cute log to write notes in and Mike surprised me with a love note 😍

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794068751/jiejg8e9fpc2swz2uzqk.jpg)

The ship started rocking the first evening as we headed into Drake’s Passage. We were marched up and down decks multiple times this evening for briefings, meals, and more briefings.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgw35sldo/image/upload/c_limit,f_auto,q_auto,w_1200/v1794069170/yangbwbh96emxynv3evv.jpg)

The staff made it very clear that this is NOT a cruise; this is an expedition. What’s the difference? The schedule can (and probably will) change. The weather here is very unpredictable! Also, our wake-up times are not in line with typical cruises. Our “sleep in” day is wake up at 745 with a prompt breakfast of 8am and will close at 9am. Activity days have a wake-up time of 7am. 

The staff gave multiple presentations so we knew what to expect and drills for emergencies. We were in for a Drake Shake, as they call it.
